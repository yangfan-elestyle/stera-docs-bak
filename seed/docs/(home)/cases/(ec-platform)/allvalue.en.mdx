title: AllValue
---

1.  Log in to the AllValue admin panel and click "Settings" → "Payments".

![](/docs/413149c-pasted-image.png)

2.  On the "Payments" page, under "Other payment methods," click the "Select payment method" link.

![](/docs/f17adfb-pasted-image-2.png)

3.  From the list of payment service providers, click "elepay".

![](/docs/f71a6fc-pasted-image-3.png)

4.  On the stera smart one settings screen, enter the App ID and Secret (for the values, please contact the stera smart one support desk).

![](/docs/4de8650-pasted-image-4.png)

5.  Specify the payment methods available for users to choose. When finished, click the "Save" button.

![](/docs/c94d8d3-pasted-image-5.png)

6.  After returning to the Payments page, under "Checkout settings" you can adjust the display order of payment methods.

![](/docs/920b7ae-pasted-image-6.png)
