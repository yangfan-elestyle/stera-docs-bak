---
title: About Promotion (Optional)
---

You can use the promotion feature to announce campaigns and events.<br />
After configuring each item, you can upload banner or pop-up images; on the Enterprise plan, you can also set links to external/internal pages, coupons, or games.

### 1. Click "Promotion".

![](/docs/f0633c98-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click "New".

![](/docs/ecbbaa7f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Enter the promotion’s basic information: name, target stores, and period.

![](/docs/14616330-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Select the display type.

・Banner: Standard display

・Pop-up: Display options include once only, every time, or at specified day intervals

![](/docs/79d73038-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Select and upload a promotion image.

![](/docs/a9e3750c-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Configure the destination for the promotion image.

Enterprise plan users can choose to redirect to an external link, an internal link, a coupon, or a game (additional development cost required).

![](/docs/ae41cb9f-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Enter the destination link.

![](/docs/07e7a033-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Click "Save".

![](/docs/21c6d18b-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Click "Publish" to complete the setup.

![](/docs/c9c806df-ascreenshot.jpeg)
