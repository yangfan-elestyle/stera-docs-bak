---
title: About Receipt Issuance (Optional)
---

Users can download receipts (PDF) on the post‑order screen or the order history screen. Immediate issuance and re‑issuance are also supported. If you want to provide a receipt download feature or run fully paperless to reduce costs, please configure this feature.

## Administrator‑side operations

### 1. Click Marketplace.

![](/docs/4e248a0c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click "Electronic receipt issuance".

![](/docs/7bdebc31-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click "Install".

![](/docs/ad76f605-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. On the store side, configure issuer name, company seal, etc., on the "Settings" tab.

![](/docs/35769e08-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click "Confirm" to complete the settings.

![](/docs/5a8e8bba-ascreenshot.jpeg)

Alert: Receipts are not issued outside of mobile ordering. They are issued only when orders are placed on the user’s smartphone. Thank you for your understanding.

## User‑side operations

### 6. Customers use their camera to scan the store’s QR code.

![](/docs/5be3e7bc-screenshot.webp)

### 7. Customers tap the product they want to order on their smartphone.

![](/docs/36d362f2-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Click "Pay" to proceed to checkout.

![](/docs/878b30f0-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Customers select their preferred payment method and click "Pay".

![](/docs/b4efbe92-screenshot.webp)

### 10. After payment is complete, click "Issue receipt".

![](/docs/ffe6793a-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. When the status shows Issued, click "Download".

![](/docs/e071214d-screenshot.webp)

Tip: If you set the Qualified Invoice Issuer Registration Number in the consumption tax feature, the number will appear on receipts and invoices.

### 12. This is a sample of the downloaded receipt.

![](/docs/611012f5-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
