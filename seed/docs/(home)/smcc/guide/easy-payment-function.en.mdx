This section explains how to use the Invoice Payment function in the Easy Payment feature.

## How to Create a New Invoice

### 1. In App Management, click 「elepay」.

![](/docs/0c2a5472-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click “Easy Payment”.

![](/docs/62796c1c-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click “Invoice Payment”.

![](/docs/30200412-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click “New”.

![](/docs/d3b07be6-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Enter the required invoice information and click “Save”.

### 1. For new customers, click “＋New Customer” and enter the customer’s contact information.

### 1. For existing customers, select from the customer list or use search to enter the customer name; auto search will start.

![](/docs/5190980b-screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. After saving the invoice, click “Send” to deliver it to the customer. Review the invoice and click “OK”.
