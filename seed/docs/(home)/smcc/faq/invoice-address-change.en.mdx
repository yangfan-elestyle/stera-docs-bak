---
title: How do I change the email address for receiving invoices?
---

## The email address for receiving invoices can be changed from the admin console.

1.Log in to the admin console

2.From the Invoice Management menu, click 【Invoice Settings】

3.Click ［Notification Settings］

4.Select ＜Customize＞

5.Enter the email address of the person who will receive invoices in either To or Cc

6.Click 「Save」
