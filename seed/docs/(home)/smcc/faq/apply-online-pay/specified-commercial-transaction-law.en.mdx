---
title: What Is the “Notation Based on the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions”?
---

The Act on Specified Commercial Transactions is a law established to prevent illegal sales practices and malicious solicitations by sellers in sales formats prone to consumer issues, such as door-to-door sales and online sales.

When operating an online site, you are required to publish “Information required under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions” on your website. Place it where it is easily accessible from the shop site’s top page.

This requirement also applies to individuals; you must provide actual information such as your name, address, and phone number.

**If your site does not provide the “Information required under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions,” we cannot conduct the review even if you apply. Please make sure to prepare it.**
