---
title: Do merchants need to prepare anything for the application?
---

The required items at the time of application differ for sole proprietors and corporations.

#### For Sole Proprietors

One of the following identity documents:

- Driver’s license or Driving History Certificate
- Residence Card or Special Permanent Resident Certificate
- Individual Number Card (My Number Card)
- Health insurance card

The name and date of birth entered in the application form must match those on the identity document.

One photo each of the following store images:

- Exterior photo of the store (capture a full view so that the store name on signage and the entrance are clearly visible)
- Interior photo of the store (capture a full view that conveys the services offered and the merchandise handled)

#### For Corporations

- Corporate Number (13‑digit number issued upon corporate registration)
  - You can search Corporate Numbers on the National Tax Agency’s Corporate Number Publication site.

One of the following identity documents for the contact person (only if applying for credit card payments):

- Driver’s license or Driving History Certificate
- Residence Card or Special Permanent Resident Certificate
- Individual Number Card (My Number Card)
- Health insurance card

One photo each of the following store images:

- Exterior photo of the store (capture a full view so that the store name on signage and the entrance are clearly visible)
- Interior photo of the store (capture a full view that conveys the services offered and the merchandise handled)

| Insurance (insurance agency)                                  | Agency commission agreement or insurance solicitor registration cards/certificates for two persons                  |
| Real estate brokerage                                         | Real Estate Transaction Agent license or certified copy of the corporate registry (full record)                     |
| Entertainment ticket sales                                    | Secondhand Dealer Permit (for secondary sales) / Agreement to comply with the Act on Prohibition of Ticket Resale※3 |

| Law firm                                                      | ID card issued by the Japan Federation of Bar Associations                                                          |
| Local government                                              | Agreement concerning municipal revenue (PayPay)                                                                     |

※1 Specified Continuous Services: cram schools, home tutoring, language schools, esthetics, cosmetic medicine, PC schools, matchmaking services, etc.  
※2 Required if you are not the organizer.  
※3 After we accept your application, we will provide our prescribed format separately.  
※For business types not listed above, a legal qualification may be required. We may request additional documents after application; thank you for your understanding.
