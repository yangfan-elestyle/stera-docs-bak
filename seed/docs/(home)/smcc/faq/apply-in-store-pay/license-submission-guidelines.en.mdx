---
title: Notes on submitting permits
---

If you are applying for a business category that requires permits/certificates, you must submit the relevant permits. Please prepare the required permits in advance.

For applications in business categories that require permits/certificates, you will be asked to submit details of those permits. Please have the necessary permits at hand (e.g., business operation permit, Secondhand Dealer Permit, Certificate of Notification of Establishment, Travel Agency registration certificate, qualification certificates, certification/registration numbers, prefectural authorization numbers, etc.).

### Examples of required permits/certificates

- Business operation permit
- Secondhand Dealer Permit
- Certificate of Notification of Establishment
- Travel Agency registration certificate
- Qualification certificate
- Certification/registration number
- Prefectural authorization number, etc.

### Examples of business types that require permits (if applicable)

| Business type / products handled                              | Required documents                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Handles used/recycled goods or secondhand articles            | Secondhand Dealer Permit                                                                                                                             |
| Falls under treatment facilities (acupuncture, clinics, etc.) | Certificate of Notification of Establishment                                                                                                         |
| Travel agency                                                 | Travel Agency registration certificate                                                                                                               |
| Provides medical or legal consultations                       | Qualification/registration certificate (e.g., Social Worker, Mental Health Social Worker, Attorney ID, etc.)                                         |
| Driving agency service                                        | Certification for Automobile Driving Agency Business                                                                                                 |
| Specified Continuous Services※1                               | Corporation: financial statements (last three fiscal years) / Sole proprietor: blue return financial statements (3 years) + copy of final tax return |
| Lottery / public competitions                                 | Copy of sales agency contract from the organizer※2                                                                                                   |
| Insurance (insurance agency)                                  | Agency commission agreement or insurance solicitor registration cards/certificates for two persons                                                   |
| Entertainment ticket sales                                    | Secondhand Dealer Permit (for secondary sales) / Agreement to comply with the Act on Prohibition of Unjustifiable Ticket Resale※3                    |
| Gift cards, prepaid cards, transport tickets sales            | Secondhand Dealer Permit (for secondary sales)                                                                                                       |

※1 Specified Continuous Services: cram schools, home tutoring, language schools, esthetics, cosmetic medicine, PC schools, matchmaking services, etc.  
※2 Required if you are not the organizer.  
※3 After we accept your application, we will provide our prescribed format separately.

### Restricted business categories and stores

We do not accept applications from the following business categories:

- Illegal sellers
- Adult entertainment businesses
- Gambling-related businesses
- Other categories deemed inappropriate by our company

For details, please refer to the Terms of Use and the Guidelines.
