elepay is a mobile‑focused multi‑payment platform. It consolidates complex mobile payment processing, enabling support for major mobile payment methods with a single integration. The elepay SDK includes a Client SDK and a Server SDK. The Client SDK supports iOS, Android, and HTML5. The Server SDK supports seven backend programming languages.

# Target

## Startup Support

Start using the service while keeping the costs of developing, operating, and managing your payment system to a minimum.

## E-commerce Services

For e‑commerce operators who buy and sell online, you can reduce lost purchase opportunities caused by a limited set of payment options.

## Inbound

Supports mobile payment methods commonly used abroad, such as Alipay, WechatPay, UnionPay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Paypal. This helps services that drive spending in Japan by inbound visitors.

## Sharing Economy

We provide optimal payment solutions for sharing‑economy services such as bike sharing, battery sharing, and parking sharing.

## O2O Services

Provide QR code solutions that connect online services (Online) to customer acquisition and purchase promotion at physical stores (Offline).
