Elepay is a multi-payment platform specialized for mobile. It unifies complex mobile payment processing, enabling support for major mobile payment methods with a single development effort. The elepay SDK includes both Client SDK and Server SDK. The Client SDK supports iOS, Android, and HTML5. The Server SDK supports 7 backend development languages.

# Features

## Easy Integration

- Unified SDK/API
- Developer-friendly experience
- Simple and efficient integration
- Excellent API design

## All-in-One

Support major mobile payment methods with a single development effort. We plan to continue adding support for new mobile payment methods.

| Region | Available Payment Brands |
| ------ | ------------------------ |
| Japan | PayPay, LINE Pay, Rakuten Pay, au Pay, d Payment, Paidy, atone, AEON Pay |
| Mainland China | Alipay, WeChat Pay, UnionPay, QuickPass |
| Asia | JKOPAY (Taiwan), AlipayHK (Hong Kong), KaKaoPay (South Korea), Touch'n Go (Malaysia), GCash (Philippines), TrueMoney (Thailand), DANA (Indonesia), Rabbit LINE Pay, Boost, BPI |
| Global | Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, AmazonPay, Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, JCB, DinnersClub, Discover) |

# Target Use Cases

## Startup Support

Start your service while minimizing the costs of developing, operating, and managing payment systems.

## E-commerce Services

For e-commerce business owners selling goods online, reduce lost purchasing opportunities due to limited payment options.

## Inbound Tourism

Support mobile payment methods commonly used abroad such as Alipay, WeChat Pay, UnionPay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. Helpful for services that facilitate consumption activities by foreign visitors in Japan.

## Sharing Economy

We provide optimal payment solutions for sharing economy services such as bike sharing, battery sharing, and parking sharing.

## O2O Services

We provide QR code solutions that connect online services to offline stores, enhancing customer acquisition and purchase promotion.
