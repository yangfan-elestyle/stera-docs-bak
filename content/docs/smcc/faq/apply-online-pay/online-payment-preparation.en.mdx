title: Preparation for Online Payment Review
---

To adopt payment brands, merchants must pass brand-specific onboarding reviews. While the detailed criteria are not publicly disclosed, based on available information this page outlines what should be prepared before submitting an application.

**Required documents**  
Required items vary by payment brand and the goods you sell, but the main documents are:

- Qualification certificate(s)
- Permit(s) for regulated services or products, if applicable
- Copy of the commercial registry record (for sole proprietors, an ID and a copy of the business opening filing)
- Bank account information for settlement

**E-commerce cart functionality**  
Customers must be able to browse items and add them to a shopping cart.
