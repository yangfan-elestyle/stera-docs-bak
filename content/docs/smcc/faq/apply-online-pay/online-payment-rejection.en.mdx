---
title: Industries and Products Likely to Fail Online Payment Reviews
---

In merchant reviews conducted by payment brands, factors such as industry, services offered, and products handled are assessed. The industries and products listed below are prone to negative evaluation and therefore have a higher likelihood of being rejected. Because detailed reasons for rejection are generally not disclosed, we strongly recommend checking whether any of these apply before you submit an application.

Industries:  
・ Adult content/services  
・ Marriage agencies  
・ Gambling  
・ Door-to-door sales  
・ Dating sites  
・ Ongoing/continuity services (e.g., beauty salons, English conversation schools)  
・ Industries contrary to public order and morals  
・ Crowdfunding  
・ Donation acceptance

Products:  
・ Water purifiers  
・ Water heaters  
・ Kimono (traditional garments)  
・ Paintings  
・ Down comforters  
・ Medical devices  
・ Educational materials  
・ Fur products  
・ Aesthetic/beauty-salon-related products  
・ Membership rights  
・ Jewelry  
・ Fire extinguishers  
・ Products contrary to public order and morals
