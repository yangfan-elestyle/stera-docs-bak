---
title: More than two weeks have passed since the application, but no review result has been issued. Is it possible to expedite the review?
visibleOnTenant:
  - smcc
---

Because the review process is conducted by payment brands and credit card companies, it cannot be expedited. Typically, it takes about two weeks to one month from application to notification of the result, though in some cases it may take longer.  
You can begin using any payment method as soon as its review has been completed. If you are in a hurry, please consider starting with the payment methods that have already passed review and are available.  
Please note that we are unable to provide detailed information about the progress or the specifics of the review.  
Also note that if you change or correct your application details during or after the review, a new application will be required and the review period will be extended, so please take care.
