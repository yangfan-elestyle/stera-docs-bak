We ask customers to submit store photos to confirm the existence of the store and to verify the products/services offered. When taking photos, please pay attention to the following points.

**Contents**

First photo: Exterior (composition including signage that shows the store name)

Second photo: Interior (composition that conveys the services provided)

Third photo: Interior (composition that conveys pricing)

**Common conditions**

・Landscape orientation (4:3 size recommended)

・Keep the camera level

・Clear images (without excessive color correction)

Please refer to the following PayPay examples when submitting photos.

[PayPay example: exterior image](https://paypay.ne.jp/store/introduction/paypay-outside/)  
[PayPay example: interior image](https://paypay.ne.jp/store/introduction/paypay-inside/)
