---
title: Applying before opening
---

You can apply for the service even before your store opens.  
The required materials are as follows. To facilitate a smooth review, please submit as many materials as possible even prior to opening.

Store information  
　　　　・Website (URLs for services like Tabelog or Hot Pepper are also acceptable)  
　　　　・Social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, LINE, etc.)  
　　　　・URL showing event participation information and track record (for event pop‑ups)  
Store photos  
　　　　・Exterior including the store‑name signage (photos taken during construction are acceptable if other materials can substantiate the business)  
　　　　・Interior photos (design drawings, lease agreement, building permit, or photos during construction are also acceptable)  
　　　　・Shop cards, flyers, opening notices or posters  
　　　　・Price lists, menus, or product photos with price tags

If the submitted business materials are insufficient, we may request additional documentation.  
When additional materials are requested, please submit them as soon as they are ready.
