---
title: What kind of store images should we prepare?
---

Please prepare three photos: exterior, interior, and price list.

To confirm the existence of the store and the merchandise handled, we ask you to submit store photos.  
When taking photos, please observe the following points.

### Composition

First: Exterior (include signage that shows the store name)  
Second: Interior (photo that conveys the store’s services)  
Third: Price list (photo that conveys service pricing)

### Common conditions

- Landscape orientation (4:3 size recommended)
- Keep the camera level
- Clear images (without excessive color adjustment)

Please refer to the following PayPay pages when submitting images.

- [PayPay example: exterior image](https://paypay.ne.jp/store/introduction/paypay-outside/)
- [PayPay example: interior image](https://paypay.ne.jp/store/introduction/paypay-inside/)
