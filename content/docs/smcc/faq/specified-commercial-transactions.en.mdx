---
title: About the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions (Mail-order, Door-to-door Sales, etc.)
---

This is a comprehensive guide to Japan’s Act on Specified Commercial Transactions (ASCT). It concisely explains the legal regulations that apply to various transaction types such as mail-order sales, door-to-door sales, and telephone solicitation sales, providing the knowledge necessary for both consumers and businesses to conduct safe and fair transactions.

**Overview**  
The ASCT is a law designed to ensure that consumers can purchase goods and services with confidence. Focusing on sales methods and transaction types where problems frequently occur, the Act aims to prevent unfair solicitation and protect consumer interests.

**Major Transaction Types**  
**Mail-order Sales**: Transactions advertised via newspapers, magazines, the Internet, etc., where orders are placed using postal mail, telephone, or other means of communication.  
**Door-to-door Sales**: Transactions in which goods or services are offered by visiting a consumer’s home or other location. However, cases where a contract has already been concluded in advance are excluded.  
**Telephone Solicitation Sales**: Transactions in which orders are solicited by telephone; cases where an order is placed after the call ends are also included.  
**Chain Sales Transactions**: Transactions that recruit individuals as salespersons and have those salespersons recruit additional salespersons in turn.  
**Business Opportunity Sales Transactions**: Transactions that, under the pretense of “providing work,” sell goods/services and require a monetary burden.  
**Specified Continuous Services**: Transactions involving the provision of services over a long period together with an agreement to pay a high consideration. Examples include:  
a. Esthetic services  
b. Cosmetic medical services  
c. Language schools  
d. Home tutoring  
e. Cram schools  
f. Matchmaking services  
g. Personal computer classes

**Compliance Notes**  
Particular care is required if, within the past five years, you have been subject to a disposition under the ASCT or have received an adverse civil judgment for violating the Consumer Contract Act.

**Further Information**  
For more detailed information on the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions, refer to the Consumer Affairs Agency’s guide: [Act on Specified Commercial Transactions](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/consumer_transaction/). There you can find details of the law and specific explanations for each transaction type.
