This section explains how to use the invoice payment feature in the payment module.<br />
You can issue invoices to corporate or individual customers and accept payments via the attached QR code.

## Create a new invoice

### 1. In "App management", click "Payment module".

![](/docs/ab8a7430-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click Easy Payment.

![](/docs/07b7d58d-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click "Invoice payment".

![](/docs/9e907c01-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click "New".

![](/docs/10eebff0-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Enter the information required to create an invoice and click Save.

For a new customer, click "+ New customer" and enter their contact information.

For an existing customer, select from the customer list or search by name using the search function.

![](/docs/508ee606-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. After saving the invoice, click "Send" to send it to the customer. Review the invoice contents and click "OK".

![](/docs/15eec875-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Once the invoice is finalized, clicking "Send" lets you deliver it to the customer in one of three ways. Choose the method that fits your operations and click "OK".

1. Copy URL: Send the invoice link to the customer via SMS, etc., or include it in your regular newsletters.

2. Quick email: Send the invoice to the customer’s email address.

3. QR code: Send the QR image as‑is, or print the invoice (as a PDF) and send it to the customer.

![](/docs/aa9fe2d0-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 8. When prompted "Change invoice status to Sent?", click "OK". The status will change to "Sent".

![](/docs/dc9ea7bb-screenshot.jpeg)

## After sending the invoice, how to check payment status

### 9. Click "Invoice payment".

![](/docs/8e3b9dc3-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 10. Check the customer’s payment status in real time from the Status column.

    When the invoice status becomes "Paid", click "Details" to review the invoice details.

![](/docs/cd43c174-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. When payment is complete, the "Payment" section is added to the invoice.

You can verify the specific payment method and also process refunds.<br />
※ Refunds may be subject to each payment brand’s refund fees. Please note this in advance.

![](/docs/e1ade484-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Alert: Note

Even if a payment is refunded, the invoice status remains "Paid" and does not change to Refunded. Please be aware of this behavior.

## Guidance on expired invoices

Tip: Even if an invoice expires, to save merchants the effort of creating it again, payment can still be made in the dashboard system.

There are two approaches for expired invoices:

1. Use Copy to create a new invoice.

   A new invoice is created based on the information from the expired invoice.

2. Accept payment using the expired invoice as‑is.

Please choose the method that fits your operations.
