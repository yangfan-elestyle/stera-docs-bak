---
title: About Inventory Management (Optional)
---

By using the inventory management feature, you can easily understand product stock status and keep sales running smoothly.<br />
When stock falls below the safety stock level, you receive a notification and can confirm the appropriate timing for replenishment. In addition, the product’s “theoretical inventory” lets you check the accurate stock status at any time.<br />
This feature supports efficient sales operations without worrying about stock availability.

## How to set the safety stock level

### 1. Click "Store".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/f081d462-fbb5-460a-8030-20991a14e9df/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,578&br_px=2940,1540&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=892,287)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/4e62f2c6-4a9f-4762-827b-a980f3730604/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,113&br_px=1376,882&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=92,277)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/59576cae-582b-46d9-9873-9756ee739337/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=680,117&br_px=1827,758&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=523,277)

When the level is breached, you receive notifications via email, etc.

This enables appropriate inventory control and helps determine the right timing to replenish.

### 14. To verify the reflected stock quantities, check "Theoretical inventory".

The theoretical inventory is the quantity obtained by subtracting outgoing quantities from incoming quantities, and it indicates the actual inventory situation.
