---
title: 关于库存管理（可选）
---

通过使用库存管理功能，可轻松把握商品库存状况，保障销售顺畅进行。<br />
当库存低于安全库存数时，会收到通知以便确认合适的补货时机。此外，借助商品的「理论库存」，可随时准确了解库存状态。<br />
该功能可在无需时刻担心库存的情况下，支持高效的销售运营。

## 安全库存数设置方法

### 1. 点击「Store」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-20/219c1140-cee8-4d0c-af76-23116d63e205/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,578&br_px=1719,1540&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=112,356)

### 2. 点击「库存管理」并安装。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/f081d462-fbb5-460a-8030-20991a14e9df/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,578&br_px=2940,1540&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=892,287)

### 3. 设置缺货显示。

若希望在无库存时仍优先展示并销售商品，请禁用缺货显示。商品将始终显示为可购买状态。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/23e8f0d3-ecec-4157-a6e7-41a93bc28525/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=576,129&br_px=1952,898&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,276)

### 4. 若需为商品设置安全库存数，请点击「库存管理」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/4e62f2c6-4a9f-4762-827b-a980f3730604/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,113&br_px=1376,882&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=92,277)

### 5. 点击「设置」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/02d56827-9610-4926-ae99-811a093fb212/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,207&br_px=1719,1168&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=94,276)

### 6. 点击「安全线」，输入商品与店铺的安全库存数。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/59576cae-582b-46d9-9873-9756ee739337/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=680,117&br_px=1827,758&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=523,277)

### 7. 商品安全库存数用于在库存下降到一定水平时发出预警。

当低于安全库存数时，会通过邮件等方式发送通知。

据此可实现恰当的库存管理并把握补货时机。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/133ff887-e509-469d-a41b-3c8f1e4f0824/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=772,151&br_px=2492,1112&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,276)

### 8. 点击「保存」，完成安全库存设置。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/8379594c-aa81-44dc-bcb4-c26f2943b777/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=388,578&br_px=2108,1540&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,339)

## 店铺盘点设置与确认方法

### 9. 点击「店铺库存」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/df1644d4-133a-431f-8049-2137202c8193/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,117&br_px=1719,1078&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=43,277)

### 10. 在需查看的店铺右侧点击「详情」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/7fd81a3b-c91c-48fb-adac-8ffec426a0ea/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=646,83&br_px=2940,1364&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=996,277)

### 11. 点击「盘点」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/caf9bebe-0319-4518-93b0-aa84233ab0c4/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,0&br_px=2940,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=758,219)

### 12. 输入相关商品的库存数量。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/896f0d12-56cb-467d-acf4-70a9778ebe4c/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=646,205&br_px=2940,1486&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=606,277)

### 13. 点击「保存」，完成设置。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/5587ea4b-ca9c-4726-a847-8fd9a9cd7513/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,0&br_px=2940,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=988,225)

### 14. 如需核对已反映的库存数量，请查看「理论库存」。

所谓理论库存，是指入库量减去出库量后的数量，是反映实际库存状况的重要指标。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/3ffe8f97-61c6-4a56-8c37-782fea310959/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=400,207&br_px=2120,1168&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,276)
