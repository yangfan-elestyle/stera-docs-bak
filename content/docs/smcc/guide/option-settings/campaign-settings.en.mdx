① Product campaigns allow you to set a percentage discount (%), an amount discount (JPY), or a specific price for certain products or product groups.<br />
② Order campaigns allow you to grant benefits based on specific ordering methods or order amounts.

## How to set up a product campaign

Tip: A product campaign is applied to specific products or multiple products.<br />
Select the campaign period, target stores, and target products, and set per‑item discounts or special prices.

### 1. Click "Campaign".

![](/docs/f32afb98-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click "New".

![](/docs/eb48feb4-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Enter the "Campaign name".

![](/docs/0ae7c4ce-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Select "Product campaign" and set the campaign type.

![](/docs/ed70aebd-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click "Save".

![](/docs/1886bc09-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. You can set discount types: percentage (%), amount discount (JPY), or fixed price (specific price).

![](/docs/73f8fba0-screenshot.webp)

### 7. Configure basic information, period, and time windows for the campaign.

![](/docs/3370a71b-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Select the target stores and target products for the campaign.

![](/docs/8376e3ad-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 9. After confirming everything, click "OK".

![](/docs/2c0570a8-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 10. Click "Save" to complete the setup.

![](/docs/f8fadbe2-ascreenshot.jpeg)

## How to set up an order campaign

Tip: An order campaign is applied at specific ordering times or based on the order amount.<br />
This campaign allows you to apply discounts based on purchase quantity or total amount.<br />
Set the campaign period, target stores, and the eligible order conditions.

### 11. As with product campaigns, click "Campaign" then "New", and enter the campaign name.

Then click "Order campaign".

![](/docs/2b7b888b-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 12. Under "Type", you can configure percentage or amount discounts.

![](/docs/17aba281-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 13. You can also restrict eligible payment methods.

If no specific method is required, select "All".

![](/docs/34d09121-screenshot.jpeg)

### 14. Specify the "Campaign period" and set an end date if needed.

![](/docs/e3eacd99-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 15. Specify the "Target stores".

![](/docs/60591bc6-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 16. Click "Save" to complete the setup.

![](/docs/b01f5c0d-ascreenshot.jpeg)
