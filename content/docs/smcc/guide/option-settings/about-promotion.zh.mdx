---
title: 关于促销（可选）
---

可以使用促销功能发布活动与事件公告。<br />
完成各项设置后，可上传横幅或弹窗图片；在企业版中，还可以设置跳转到外部/内部页面、优惠券或游戏（需另行开发费用）。

### 1. 点击「促销」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/295febca-07b5-4aea-84ed-e6d82426eddf/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,235&br_px=1376,1004&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=165,276)

### 2. 点击「新建」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/8a21dc9a-1926-4831-9d04-84865867dfde/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,0&br_px=2940,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=923,264)

### 3. 输入促销的基本信息：名称、目标店铺与期间。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/6994ec3b-a356-419b-b335-3c2004909fa2/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=668,0&br_px=2388,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,203)

### 4. 选择显示类型。

・横幅：常规展示

・弹窗：可选择仅显示一次、每次显示，或按指定天数间隔显示

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/7eea2547-5f65-45a0-9019-f75f08073037/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=796,227&br_px=2516,1188&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=523,276)

### 5. 选择并上传促销图片。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/952c86d0-4a93-468f-9e0d-967856641e00/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=730,449&br_px=2450,1410&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=523,276)

### 6. 配置促销图片的跳转目标。

企业版用户可选择跳转到外部链接、内部链接、优惠券或游戏（需另行开发费用）。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/d867c301-7370-435a-8b6f-36055646fabc/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=741,504&br_px=2461,1466&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,355)

### 7. 输入跳转链接。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/0d5801fa-1258-4e32-8654-759b9e04ef77/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=744,504&br_px=2464,1466&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,432)

### 8. 点击「保存」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/6ea386c3-c89b-4e4d-bb53-42947876942d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=624,504&br_px=2344,1466&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,501)

### 9. 点击「发布」，完成设置。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/4c8c8f32-7f3a-4a92-ae01-98942fda4141/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=810,504&br_px=2530,1466&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,534)
