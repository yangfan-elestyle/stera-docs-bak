You can configure product/service tax rates and the Qualified Invoice Issuer Registration Number here.<br />
When adding a new product, the preset default tax rate is applied automatically.<br />
If you select "Auto apply" for "Tax rate application", the standard rate is applied to regular products and the reduced rate to takeout items, respectively.

Alert: In this system, prices displayed to users are tax‑inclusive. Even when external‑tax settings are used, the final display is a tax‑inclusive price based on the pre‑tax amount.

Tax‑inclusive amounts are calculated from the specified tax rate and rounding method. As a result of rounding, the final product price (tax‑included) may differ from the value configured in the admin screen. Please keep this in mind and verify it when configuring settings and prices.

## Merchant‑side settings

### 1. Click Marketplace.

![](/docs/29579547-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Install "Consumption tax settings".

![](/docs/55d5ed02-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Configure tax settings.

If you use the system outside retail or food service industries, tax‑inclusive prices are automatically set with the 10% standard rate. In that case, no additional settings for tax type, applicable rate, or rounding are required.

![](/docs/e37fd180-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Select "Applicable tax rate" and "Rounding method" from the options.

If you choose "Auto apply" for "Applicable tax rate", products default to the standard rate and takeout items to the reduced rate automatically.

![](/docs/69cb1831-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Set the Qualified Invoice Issuer Registration Number.

When you register the number, it appears on receipts and invoices.

![](/docs/9ea35cdb-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Click "Save" to complete the setup.

![](/docs/06cfe386-ascreenshot.jpeg)
