This guide provides a concise overview of product setup and editing, order review, refund processing, and store configuration. It also explains how to generate dedicated QR codes and print the guide.

## Log In to the Admin Console

### 1. An invitation email will be sent to your registered address. Log in to the admin console from the email you receive.

![](/docs/f60f7ef9-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Tip: The invitation email is valid for 48 hours. Please note.

### 2. Click '確認する' in the invitation email.

![](/docs/d8da0c61-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Set your account name and password, then click 'アカウント追加'.

![](/docs/2f0498d0-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## Create a Store

### 4. After logging in, confirm that the app for your legal entity or business name is displayed. For the entity/storefront where you want to add a store, click 'SaaSサービス'.

![](/docs/06532e29-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. From the menu, select '店舗'. You can also create a new store.

![](/docs/1cabe4cf-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. From the menu, choose '店舗' > '店舗一覧', then click '＋新規' to enter the new store information.

![](/docs/613a6edc-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Enter the store information (store name, address, etc.).

After entering the postal code, click '住所自動入力' to automatically populate the address.

![](/docs/208a8512-stack_animation.webp)

### 8. Click '保存' to save the store information.

Click '公開する' to publish the store; its dedicated QR code will be activated.

![](/docs/3a8c7c94-stack_animation.webp)

### 9. **Publishing the store by selecting '公開する' will incur the full monthly fee for the current month.**

**Proration is not applied; publish a store with care.**

![](/docs/efba55c3-ascreenshot.jpeg)

## Register and Publish Products

### 10. From the menu, select '商品' > '商品一覧'.

![](/docs/056eb2a1-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Click '＋新規' and enter the new product information (product name, price, etc.).

![](/docs/046ea09e-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 12. Configure product details such as name and price.

![](/docs/bdf60e87-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 13. Select a category from the Category field to group products.

To add a new category, use the 'カテゴリー' sub-menu.

![](/docs/d336d880-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 14. Add a label: Click '+ラベルを選択する' to choose a label.

![](/docs/d8651b2f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 15. Select a label.

The selected label appears on the edge of the product image, which is useful for highlighting specific products.

![](/docs/7a97ec1a-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 16. Set product details such as price and applicable tax rate.

    From the pull-down, select the applicable tax rate for the product.

![](/docs/ab2bdbb4-stack_animation.webp)

### 17. Click '保存' to save the product information.

Click '公開する' to publish the product.

![](/docs/d5833841-stack_animation.webp)

## Configure Items for Sale

### 18. In the menu, click '店舗' > '店舗一覧'.

![](/docs/c4c165f5-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 19. Open '詳細' for the store where you want to sell.

![](/docs/03653cd7-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 20. From the '商品設定' tab, select '販売商品管理'.

![](/docs/a0516b3b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 21. Click '販売商品管理'.

![](/docs/cacf99bb-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 22. Check the products you want to sell and select 'OK' to make them available for selection at that store.

![](/docs/8ec1f808-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 23. You can change the order in which products appear to customers. In the '商品設定' tab, click '並び替え'.

![](/docs/41de0b7c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 24. Drag and drop to reorder products, then save.

![](/docs/cbaf7861-stack_animation.webp)

## Print Store QR Codes

### 25. Click '店舗'.

![](/docs/a9d759c5-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 26. Choose the '店舗' menu and click the QR code icon for the store you want to create a QR code for.

![](/docs/701468bb-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 27. Select 'プリント' to print the QR code.

![](/docs/65cf303a-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 28. You can also print a QR code with usage instructions.

![](/docs/62b0ad38-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 29. Click 'プリント'.

Orders can be placed via printed or saved QR codes.

![](/docs/8086fbe7-ascreenshot.jpeg)

## Process Order Refunds

### 30. From the '注文' menu, display the order list.

![](/docs/3c79df9b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 31. Select the order to refund and click '返金'.

![](/docs/46394a8d-ascreenshot.jpeg)

Alert: Process refunds carefully in accordance with your merchant refund policy. Refunds are allowed within 30 days from the order date. After that, refunds may not be possible.

### 32. Set the refund amount and reason, then confirm.

    The refund amount is free-form, so partial refunds are supported.

![](/docs/4045335c-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 33. After the refund, confirm that the status changes to '返金済み'.

![](/docs/dbd4d3ac-ascreenshot.jpeg)
