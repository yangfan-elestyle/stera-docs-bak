This section explains account "Registration Information", "Payouts", "Invoice Management", and "Team Management".

## Editing Merchant Registration Information

Tip: You can edit your merchant basic information and contact details. If there are any changes, please update them here.

### 1. Click "Registration Information".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-20/758da5fd-f5e2-4349-acb6-a5af263738ac/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1376,769&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=115,216)

### 2. Edit contact person information

If the information of the contact person specified at the time of application has changed, please update it to the latest contact details. Important information such as service updates and invoice issuance will be sent to the updated contact.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-26/791ffe04-a65d-4a2d-991b-c239fc514e8b/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=44,0&br_px=2338,1281&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1018,226)

## Team Management

Tip: You can add and edit account administrators.<br />
You can also configure each administrator's permissions here.

### 8. Click "Team Management".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-26/603aedc4-9d81-420f-9321-bbf1279d2ee7/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,123&br_px=1719,1084&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=151,277)

### 9. To add a new administrator, click "New".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-26/d221cf07-f2f8-40c6-9d92-1e622c18c04a/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=606,0&br_px=2326,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,141)

### 10. Enter the email address and select "Role".

For merchant administrators, select "Corporate Administrator".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-26/71eaf5cf-b2ce-4fa4-9872-fd57d689b4a3/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=68,0&br_px=1787,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,214)

### 11. Click "+ Edit App".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/8678b92d-6c3d-457d-8c00-9c0142a33ee6/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=140,247&br_px=1860,1208&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,277)

### 12. Select the service (app) to which you want to grant permissions, then click "Invite".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-26/f358121f-f159-4a1e-b8c4-cc77ff963fef/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=52,488&br_px=1771,1450&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,491)

Tip: Post-invitation process (recipient)

### 1. An invitation email will be sent to the new administrator at the entered email address.

### 2. Click "Register" in the invitation email.

### 3. Set a password and enter profile information.

### 4. After clicking "Register" and completing the registration, the new administrator can access the dashboard.
