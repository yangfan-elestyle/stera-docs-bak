title: 快速入门指南
---

本指南将简要说明如何进行商品的设置与编辑、订单确认、退款处理以及门店设置。还将介绍如何生成专用二维码以及打印本指南。

## 登录管理后台

### 1. 我们会向您注册的邮箱发送邀请邮件。请通过收到的邮件登录管理后台。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/0fde211f-49f3-4c2a-a844-e2ce87b79e93/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=154,0&br_px=1873,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,137)

Tip: 邀请邮件的有效期为 48 小时，请注意查收并及时操作。

### 2. 点击邀请邮件中的「確認する」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-08/eb3da57c-c8cb-435e-aa8a-2c076fdcc9d6/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=818,530&br_px=2538,1492&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,329)

### 3. 设置账户名和密码，然后点击「アカウント追加」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-07-04/7859e62e-cc4f-4727-aa4c-e3cfc8324d95/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=388,397&br_px=2108,1358&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,276)

## 创建门店

### 4. 登录后，确认显示了以贵公司法人名或商号命名的应用。在需要新增门店的法人/商号下点击「SaaSサービス」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-07-04/c523bc28-c884-4f1a-9751-bd35cc11acdf/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=26,0&br_px=1745,714&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 5. 从菜单选择「店舗」，也可以新建门店。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/47dc865e-67d9-4315-921d-bc6b78048fc5/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=98,150)

### 6. 从菜单选择「店舗」＞「店舗一覧」，点击「＋新規」输入新门店信息。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/da1f7d0c-4504-468a-9cca-6a02571e9de6/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,0&br_px=2940,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=932,267)

### 7. 输入门店信息（门店名、地址等）。

输入邮政编码后，点击「住所自動入力」可自动填充地址。

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/e544b509-87f9-480b-9a89-685f33e0c6de/stack_animation.webp)

### 8. 点击「保存」完成门店信息保存。

点击「公開する」即可发布门店，专用二维码将被激活。

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/af5816cc-1093-478f-81a6-8d131aa2efbc/stack_animation.webp)

### 9. **选择「公開する」并发布门店后，当月月费将全额计费。**

**不进行按日计费，请谨慎发布门店。**

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/c69d14ba-c42f-45c9-a1dc-0e18e4b11c54/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1048,423&br_px=2768,1384&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=523,276)

## 商品的注册与发布

### 10. 从菜单选择「商品」＞「商品一覧」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/396db347-67ec-4dd9-af22-423bdce1dc5e/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,7&br_px=1719,968&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=113,277)

### 11. 点击「＋新規」，输入新商品信息（商品名、价格等）。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/4435eeea-59b4-49f2-b6f2-199f428f98ae/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,0&br_px=2940,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=953,195)

### 12. 配置商品详情，如名称与价格。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/0e80130b-ef02-4ef5-bc20-dd3c4c8168ca/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=174,347&br_px=1893,1308&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,276)

### 13. 在「カテゴリー」栏选择类别，可对商品进行分组。

如需新增类别，可在子菜单「カテゴリー」中添加。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/d1519b0c-044e-4525-b5c4-a6b10c26cf48/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=172,211&br_px=1891,1172&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,277)

### 14. 添加标签：点击「+ラベルを選択する」选择标签。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-07-04/267ed512-7a8d-49b5-8635-6c14b6a52edc/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=318,0&br_px=2038,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,68)

### 15. 选择标签。

所选标签将显示在商品图片边缘，可用于突出特定商品。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/20b1b9ab-05e9-4d43-8b80-d5f2d7979611/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=254,103&br_px=1973,1064&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,276)

### 16. 设置商品详情，如价格与适用税率。

    请从下拉列表中选择该商品的适用税率。

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/cf03776f-e61a-41fe-848c-fabbb89da5fe/stack_animation.webp)

### 17. 点击「保存」保存商品信息。

点击「公開する」发布该商品。

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/538f29b0-077a-4610-9262-342b3b14df67/stack_animation.webp)

## 销售商品的设置

### 18. 在菜单点击「店舗」＞「店舗一覧」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/7b54b68e-e075-4a4b-a79a-7c075e675bd4/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=55,167)

### 19. 进入需销售门店的「詳細」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/c576fb4e-f55c-4de8-96dd-43f7acbe2549/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,437&br_px=2940,1398&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=939,277)

### 20. 在「商品設定」选项卡中选择「販売商品管理」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/e3d3a5ca-35e6-4c91-b238-bd0a5313a92c/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=8,0&br_px=1727,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,111)

### 21. 点击「販売商品管理」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-07-04/6f81d5df-0370-417b-be30-32ab41f3238d/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=810,0&br_px=2530,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,111)

### 22. 勾选要销售的商品，选择「OK」后，即可在该门店实际选择该商品。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/5e6eea8e-82a2-4c5e-abd9-55adc92bf1a8/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,530&br_px=1719,1492&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=336,277)

### 23. 可调整展示给顾客的商品顺序。在「商品設定」选项卡中点击「並び替え」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-07-04/398e3e37-232a-4ebf-b4ca-325d82b1eb66/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=588,0&br_px=2308,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,93)

### 24. 通过拖拽调整商品顺序并保存。

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/d4d9f6b1-8af5-42a8-9ab7-d952ef263935/stack_animation.webp)

## 订单退款处理

### 30. 在「注文」菜单中显示订单列表。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/03945c1e-2578-4329-8275-e33ec82ac091/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,23&br_px=1719,984&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=14,277)

### 31. 选择需要退款的订单并点击「返金」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/a49e4e9f-aa9c-4dc7-bdbc-579038b08739/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,530&br_px=2940,1492&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=987,382)

Alert: 请按照商户退款政策谨慎执行退款操作。退款期限为下单日起 30 天内。超过期限可能无法退款，请注意。

### 32. 设置退款金额与原因并确认。

    退款金额为自由输入，支持部分退款。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/ba26350d-12b8-4ad9-bc96-c6f6973e1cee/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=260,311&br_px=1979,1272&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,276)

### 33. 退款后，请确认状态变为「返金済み」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-06/9cf24e95-18e4-4afd-bdbd-c143999834ad/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,530&br_px=2940,1492&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=1&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=645,349)
