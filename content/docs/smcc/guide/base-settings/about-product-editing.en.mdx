This manual explains the product editing features. It walks through creating new categories, adding options, and configuring multilingual settings for product menu information.

## Creating product categories (if needed)

Tip: Create categories to classify products.

### 1. From the home screen, click "SaaS Services".

![](/docs/75655eb5-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click "Products".

Click "Categories".

![](/docs/c1b41a9c-stack_animation.webp)

### 3. Click "New".

![](/docs/ea8f5812-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Enter the category name you want to create.

Click "Save".<br />
Click "Publish" to complete adding the category.<br />
To adjust the order of the created categories, click "Category Management".

![](/docs/ce0d5df8-stack_animation.webp)

### 5. All category names will be listed. Click "Sort".

![](/docs/0c47889c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Reorder categories by drag and drop.

When adjusting the display order, drag the created categories to the desired position and drop them.<br />
Click "Save" to finish reordering.

![](/docs/c3e0423f-stack_animation.webp)

## Creating product options (if needed)

Tip: Create product variations or additional items.

### 7. Click "Options".

![](/docs/60d2ed1f-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Click "New".

![](/docs/56fdf204-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Enter the option name.

![](/docs/77c4ce76-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. **Enter the option "Management Name".**

![](/docs/73ce60b6-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Click the option image to upload an image.

    Also enter the option "Item Name" and "Amount".
    The parts indicated by arrows support multiple languages.
    You can add additional options from "+".

![](/docs/823cda29-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 12. **You can add a second and subsequent option items. Click "➕".**

![](/docs/924051ad-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 13. Click the image area for the option item to upload an image. Enter the "Option Item Name" and "Amount" accordingly.

![](/docs/730c1f55-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 14. Click "Save".

![](/docs/068e1719-ascreenshot.jpeg)

## Editing product details

Tip: Edit the detailed information of the registered product.

### 15. Click "Product List".

![](/docs/b6dfa000-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 16. Click "Details".

![](/docs/9b388672-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 17. Upload product images.

![](/docs/815f91f2-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 18. **Select the categories created beforehand.**

Multiple selections are available.

![](/docs/c35e99cd-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 19. You can also add labels to the product menu. Click "Change Label".

![](/docs/69373aff-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 20. Select a label and click OK to save.

![](/docs/8e4b8c64-ascreenshot.jpeg)

![](/docs/879fbe30-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
