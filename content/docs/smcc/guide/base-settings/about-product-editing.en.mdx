title: About Product Editing
---

This manual explains the product editing features. It walks through creating new categories, adding options, and configuring multilingual settings for product menu information.

## Creating product categories (if needed)

Tip: Create categories to classify products.

### 1. From the home screen, click "SaaS Services".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-20/4aaa0b68-2614-454f-8e51-a6a8c921236b/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2940,1382&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=306,113)

### 2. Click "Products".

Click "Categories".

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/2f2fdeef-38e7-464e-85dd-e5cd0ebc7416/stack_animation.webp)

### 3. Click "New".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/a11f0126-3146-4a8f-b92b-ed1eb2c12d94/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,0&br_px=2940,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=826,139)

### 4. Enter the category name you want to create.

Click "Save".<br />
Click "Publish" to complete adding the category.<br />
To adjust the order of the created categories, click "Category Management".

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/443896d8-6402-407f-82b9-1d23999192be/stack_animation.webp)

### 5. All category names will be listed. Click "Sort".

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/1fc346cd-0d70-4170-86d9-4356cafe576e/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,0&br_px=2940,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=697,132)

### 6. Reorder categories by drag and drop.

When adjusting the display order, drag the created categories to the desired position and drop them.<br />
Click "Save" to finish reordering.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/ec3f3489-6e64-43bc-ac7e-12edf5f84c4b/stack_animation.webp)
