## 订单退款处理

### 7. 点击“退款”。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-05-17/0ae9861c-8a92-4987-9eec-6589f43b310e/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=270,107&br_px=1989,1068&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,277)

提示：请按照商户退款政策谨慎处理退款。退款期限为下单之日起30天内。超过期限可能无法办理退款，请注意。

### 8. 设置退款金额和原因。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-24/cbcad9ae-373a-44a8-9bf6-ce4bb7db5285/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=238,552&br_px=1957,1514&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,483)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-25/8a953453-fb3f-4ea5-afa2-52445bcc0586/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1220,552&br_px=2940,1514&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=643,578)
