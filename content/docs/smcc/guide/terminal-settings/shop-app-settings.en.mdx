---
title: About app settings for smart one Shop
---

smart one Shop is an application that makes payments smoother and more efficient. This guide explains how to configure the app.

### 1. How to access the Settings screen

Press and hold the lower-left corner of the Home screen for 6 seconds or more. The Settings screen will appear.

### 2. Available settings:

Once you enter the Settings screen, the following options are available.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-04-26/056904bf-042e-469a-ab5f-0799e6046365/screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1080,1920&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=630)

Tip: - **Reload**: Reloads app information.

- **Upload logs**: Uploads usage history, error logs, and related information.

- **Default scanner settings**: Change the default scan settings.

- **Payment scanner settings**: Adjust scan settings related to payments.

- **Square Terminal settings**: Manage integration settings and information for Square Terminal.

- **Configure automatic change function**: Configure automatic calculation of change.

- **Check cash storage box**: Check the status and information of cash storage.

### 3. Exporting order history:

Order history: You can review past transactions and orders. You can also use the export option to save the history in CSV or PDF format.
