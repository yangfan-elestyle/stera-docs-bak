val [googlePayEnvironment](../google-pay-environment): [GooglePayEnvironment](../../-google-pay-environment/)? = null

The environment of Google Pay.

To use Google Pay, the app(apk) needs to be sent to Google for review. And it may take several steps to finish the review. During the review process, app(apk) needs to switch the environment individually.

If the app do use Google Pay, leave this field `null`.
