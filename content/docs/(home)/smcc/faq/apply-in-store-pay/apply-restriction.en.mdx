---
title: Are applications restricted for certain business types or stores?
---

In principle, any corporation or sole proprietor that operates a physical storefront can apply. Sole proprietors should apply under the address of the store.

However, we do not accept applications from the following business categories:

- Illegal sellers
- Adult entertainment businesses
- Gambling-related businesses
- Other categories deemed inappropriate by our company

For details, please refer to the Terms of Use and the Guidelines.
