---
title: Can overseas corporations or residents outside Japan apply for the service?
---

Whether you are a sole proprietor or a corporation, payment brands require a Japanese domestic address for their screening. If you live outside Japan or your operating base is overseas, please enter a Japanese address (for example, the address you use when in Japan or your family’s address in Japan).

Our services are primarily intended for people residing in Japan. We ask that those responsible for store operations and payout management have a Japanese address. Therefore, we do not accept registration or operation by persons residing outside Japan.

In addition, the payout destination account is limited to bank accounts in Japan.
