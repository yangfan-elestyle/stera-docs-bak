This manual serves as a guide to help you complete your application for stera smart one smoothly.<br />
Please fill in the required fields and proceed with your application.<br />
We will notify the screening result to your registered email address within approximately one month.

### 1. Enter Email Address

First, register your email address. We will send subsequent communications and application status updates to the email address you provide here.

After entering your email, click Send.

![](/docs/fce442ec-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Receive the Confirmation Email

A confirmation email will be sent to the address you entered. Check your inbox and spam folders, and open the email when it arrives.

![](/docs/83efde4d-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click Start Registration in the email and proceed with the application from the link.

![](/docs/f2940ca9-ascreenshot.jpeg)

Tip: **Prepare the required documents**

- For corporations: Certified copy of registry, relevant permits, licenses

- Sole proprietors: Identity document (e.g., driver's license)

- All applicants: Bank account information (deposit account number)

Tip: For in-store sales

- Prepare at least three photos of the representative store or facility (**jpg, png** ）。  
  [What kind of images should I prepare?](https://guides.sterasmartone.com/docs/%E3%81%A9%E3%82%93%E3%81%AA%E7%94%BB%E5%83%8F%E3%82%92%E7%94%A8%E6%84%8F%E3%81%99%E3%82%8B%E3%81%A8%E3%81%84%E3%81%84%E3%81%AE%E3%81%A7%E3%81%99%E3%81%8B)

### 4. Review the stera smart one Terms of Use and the consent terms for handling corporate information including personal data related to stera smart one, and check the box.

After completing the input, click Next.

![](/docs/ca58366d-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Business Type Details

Select Corporation or Sole Proprietor.<br />
Click Next.

![](/docs/f90380d4-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Click the applicable Service Type.

・SaaS service: Choose this when using not only the payment platform but also mobile/self-order and unmanned sales.

・Payment module: Choose this when you already have a sales tool and only adopt the payment component (payment platform), or when applying only for the invoicing service.

If you have any questions, please contact our sales representative.

![](/docs/92473618-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Confirm the available payment methods and click Next.

![](/docs/890c3ed5-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Business Details

Enter your business details.

Enter your postal code and click the Auto-fill Address button to populate your address automatically.

![](/docs/4894f6a6-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Enter your phone number.

Upload materials that describe your business (e.g., a menu or similar).

![](/docs/bb999fae-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. When uploading photos, select them with reference to the points on the right.

![](/docs/047523be-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Refer to the following points when preparing photos.

![](/docs/714b4c1e-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 12. Click Next.

![](/docs/1e54ade6-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 13. Company/Business Information

Enter the sole proprietor's name, kana, and romanized spelling.<br />
Enter your website URL, opening date, and postal code.

![](/docs/b4714cf4-stack_animation.webp)

### 14. Click Next.

![](/docs/46604441-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Alert: Notes on Public Information settings

・The information you submit will appear on customers' payment screens and subsequent statements.

・Register the store or service name (trade name) accurately and clearly so customers can easily recognize it.

・Public Information is required for customers to identify where they made a payment. Avoid misleading expressions and enter accurate information.

### 15. Register Public Information

Enter the trade/service name, kana, and romanized spelling.

※This information is displayed to customers at payment time. Please enter it accurately.

![](/docs/92c638a7-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 16. Click Next.

![](/docs/9dfe5560-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 17. Representative/Contact Information

If the contact person is the same as the representative, click Same as Representative.

![](/docs/b7d9b535-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 18. Enter date of birth, gender, and phone number.

![](/docs/49f7c09e-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 19. If the contact person is the same as the representative, click Same as Representative.

![](/docs/ae34fbea-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 20. Enter the email address and click Next.

![](/docs/e5da9fef-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 21. Documents to Submit

Upload photos of the representative’s identity document (front and back).

If your business requires permits, upload the relevant permits as well.

![](/docs/090aabfd-stack_animation.webp)

### 22. Click Next.

![](/docs/fb76fe33-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 23. Bank Account Information

This is the account to receive your sales proceeds.

Enter account details (financial institution, branch, account type, and account holder).

![](/docs/c58c5143-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 24. After entering the account number, click Next.

![](/docs/b7ff5b2b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 25. After entering the account number, click Next

![](/docs/be19dfda-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 26. Enter the contact person’s name, kana, and phone number, then click Next.

A final confirmation screen will appear summarizing your entries. Please check that everything is correct.

![](/docs/a2bef2ee-stack_animation.webp)

### 27. If there are no issues, click Submit.

![](/docs/0713c9e1-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 28. When this screen appears, your application is complete.

We will send the screening result to the email address you entered first.

Screening may take around one month. We appreciate your understanding.

![](/docs/2b434cbd-ascreenshot.jpeg)
