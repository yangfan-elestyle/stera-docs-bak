By using the inventory management feature, you can easily understand product stock status and keep sales running smoothly.<br />
When stock falls below the safety stock level, you receive a notification and can confirm the appropriate timing for replenishment. In addition, the product’s “theoretical inventory” lets you check the accurate stock status at any time.<br />
This feature supports efficient sales operations without worrying about stock availability.

## How to set the safety stock level

### 1. Click "Store".

![](/docs/24d3917a-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click "Inventory management" and install it.

![](/docs/def26b2f-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Configure the out‑of‑stock display.

If you want to prioritize selling products regardless of stock availability, disable the out‑of‑stock display. Products will always appear as purchasable.

![](/docs/2317c970-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 4. To set a product’s safety stock level, click "Inventory management".

![](/docs/c9af7826-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click "Settings".

![](/docs/473eba7c-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Click "Safety line" and enter the product and store safety stock levels.

![](/docs/17f63834-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. The product safety stock level triggers an alert when stock falls to a certain level.

When the level is breached, you receive notifications via email, etc.

This enables appropriate inventory control and helps determine the right timing to replenish.

![](/docs/79672631-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Click "Save" to complete the safety stock settings.

![](/docs/dbc10b6f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## Store stocktaking settings and verification

### 9. Click "Store inventory".

![](/docs/0373fe97-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. Click "Details" on the right of the store you want to check.

![](/docs/913ddbff-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Click "Stocktaking".

![](/docs/deeca8fc-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 12. Enter the stock quantities for the relevant products.

![](/docs/545fdfb9-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 13. Click "Save" to complete the settings.

![](/docs/a392cf5d-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 14. To verify the reflected stock quantities, check "Theoretical inventory".

The theoretical inventory is the quantity obtained by subtracting outgoing quantities from incoming quantities, and it indicates the actual inventory situation.

![](/docs/32a6a163-ascreenshot.jpeg)
