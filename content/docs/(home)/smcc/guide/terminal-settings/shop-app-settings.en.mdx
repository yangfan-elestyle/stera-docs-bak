---
title: About smart one Shop App Settings
---

smart one Shop is an application that enables payments to be processed more smoothly and efficiently. This guide clearly explains how to configure the app.

### 1. How to Access the Settings Screen

Press and hold the lower-left corner of the Home screen for more than 6 seconds. The settings screen will then appear.

![](/docs/c774717b-screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Available Settings:

Once you enter the settings screen, the following options are available.

![](/docs/868da767-screenshot.jpeg)

Tip: - **Reload**: Reloads the app information.

- **Upload Logs**: Uploads usage history, error logs, and related information.

- **Default Scanner Settings**: Allows you to change the default scanning settings.

- **Payment Scanner Settings**: Adjusts scanning settings related to payments.

- **Square Terminal Settings**: Manages integration settings and information for Square Terminal.

- **Configure Automatic Change**: Configures automatic calculation of change and related settings.

- **Cash Drawer Check**: Checks the status and information of cash storage.

### 3. Export Order History:

Order history: You can review past transactions and orders. You can also use the export options to save the history in CSV or PDF format.
