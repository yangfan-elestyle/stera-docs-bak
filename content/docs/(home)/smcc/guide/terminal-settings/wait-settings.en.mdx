---
title: About settings for smart one  KDS・Wait
---

This guide describes the setup procedures for smart one KDS and smart one Wait. Detailed settings for each app are configured in the merchant admin console. You can also display the same Settings screen in the app by pressing and holding the lower-left corner of the Home screen for 6 seconds or more.

## smart one KDS app settings

### 1. Click "Stores".

![](/docs/88878f09-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click "Details".

![](/docs/a83b8e81-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click "Device Management".

![](/docs/c9ddb690-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click "KDS".

![](/docs/318f663d-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click "New".

![](/docs/9b77134d-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Enter a device name.

- **Theme selection**: Choose your preferred theme.

- **Layout adjustment**: Adjust the layout to optimize what is displayed.

- **Order notification sound settings**: Set or change the order notification sound.

![](/docs/e5949422-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Click "OK".

![](/docs/2c5758e0-ascreenshot.jpeg)

## smart one Wait app settings

### 8. Click "Wait" and open the target device.

![](/docs/a9b42359-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Click "New".

![](/docs/583b5744-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. Enter a device name.

- **Theme selection**: Choose your preferred theme.

- **Layout adjustment**: Adjust the layout to optimize what is displayed.

- **Order notification sound settings**: Set or change the order notification sound.

![](/docs/f33821fd-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Click "OK" to finish.

![](/docs/7a2860b6-ascreenshot.jpeg)
