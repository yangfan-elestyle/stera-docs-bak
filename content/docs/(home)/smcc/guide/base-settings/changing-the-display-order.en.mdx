---
title: Changing the Display Order of Products and Categories
---

You can freely change the display order of products and categories to make it easier for customers to place orders.

## Change the display order of store products

### 1. Click "Store".

![](/docs/65119040-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click "Details" for the relevant store.

![](/docs/9949b44a-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click "Product Settings".

![](/docs/d9d9ae1f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click "Sort".

![](/docs/ef098b82-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Drag and drop the products up or down to change the order.

![](/docs/ac647475-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. After making changes, click "Save" to complete the settings.

![](/docs/de7fc315-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## Change the display order of product categories

### 7. Change the display order of categories

Click "Products".

![](/docs/b219ede1-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Click "Categories".

![](/docs/147cc47e-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Click "Sort".

![](/docs/306ca7d9-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. Drag and drop the product categories up or down to change the order.

![](/docs/e8c5ac42-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 11. After making changes, click "Save" to complete the settings.

![](/docs/90c777dc-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
