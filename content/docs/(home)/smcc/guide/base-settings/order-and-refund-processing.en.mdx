## Order review

### 1. Click "Orders".

![](/docs/048c3eac-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click the "End Date" field.

![](/docs/97694cb3-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click "Confirm".

![](/docs/7a73a2bb-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click "Search".

![](/docs/acb82459-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click "Details".

![](/docs/c0c56bf5-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. You can check the basic information and order amount.

![](/docs/12f704cb-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## Processing a refund for an order

### 7. Click "Refund".

![](/docs/48c46dae-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Tip: Process refunds carefully in accordance with the merchant refund policy. The refund deadline is within 30 days from the order date. After the deadline, a refund may not be possible, so please take care.

### 8. Enter the refund amount and reason.

![](/docs/5e0e5908-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Click "Refund".

![](/docs/6411fd0d-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. After the refund, make sure the status changes to "Refunded".

![](/docs/ad995578-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
