title: Marketplace - Mobile Scanner
---

Using the Mobile Scanner, users can scan in-store product barcodes with their own smartphones. When a barcode is scanned, the item is automatically added to the shopping cart, enabling a faster shopping experience and efficient item selection.

## Dashboard Settings

### 1. Click 【Marketplace】.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-04/af9a959b-38f8-4e89-bfe5-fbfce9c8ed14/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,57&br_px=2752,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=87,356)

### 2. Enter “Mobile Scanner” in the keyword field to search, then click the Mobile Scanner plugin when it appears.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-04/e649c1fe-5b17-4560-8b1e-bd2f039feabe/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2752,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=316,132)

## Operations on OneQR Mobile

### 5. 1) After completing the dashboard setup, a scanner icon floats at the bottom right of the OneQR Mobile product list page.

2) Tap the scanner icon to launch the smartphone camera.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-04/681d73a7-733a-46d4-923e-aa7160234bd8/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=782,1624&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=540)

### 6. 1) The following image shows the screen after the camera launches.

※ Depending on the smartphone OS, a popup may request camera access permission. Grant permission to use the camera.

2) Point the camera at the product JAN code to search automatically.

- If no product is found, an error message appears, but the camera remains open so you can continue scanning.
- If a product is found, the camera closes and the item is added to the cart.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-04/1ec32df2-cfb9-441b-bdd2-c5b437b5c230/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=782,1624&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=540)

### 7. Tap the 【✕】 button at the top right to close the camera.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-04/c76bee85-7f9c-4177-b4ca-1a7f60ebf6cb/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=782,1624&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=540)
