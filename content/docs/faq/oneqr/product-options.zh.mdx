---
title: Marketplace-商品选项
---

该功能用于为商品配置可自定义的选项。可设置加料、尺寸等选择项及附加费用，从而灵活扩展商品可选项，满足多样化需求并提升顾客满意度。

## 配置商品选项

### 1. 在管理后台点击【商品】。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/2e9b11af-7a48-4f2e-9a6d-c94061c7df0b/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=90,219)

### 2. 点击 \[选项\]。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/ade5a647-f4f2-4fdf-aace-2c0fca383f4e/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,183&br_px=1719,1144&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=94,277)

### 3. 点击「＋新建」创建新的选项组。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/c7e5714b-ae6b-4019-ae1b-a4a91c4e68ac/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2858,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=595,65)

### 4. 配置 \[选项名\] 与 \[管理名\]。

在管理名中可填写如米饭份量、啤酒容量等便于识别的标签。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-13/53fe44a1-da37-4762-a0bc-d7a755dbff1f/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=282,157&br_px=2575,1438&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 5. 说明文字将展示给顾客，便于下单时参考。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/3d5d6ba9-f737-4354-8d2e-4c06bf1f3b18/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,201&br_px=2293,1482&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=322,277)

### 6. 输入框右侧有地球图标，点击可配置多语言文本。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/4223fa80-fa83-4119-a812-3cd3309c81e0/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=493,0&br_px=2786,1281&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,169)

### 7. 选择所需语言后点击「OK」保存。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/f756fde0-ed61-4535-be64-513548e0e7fa/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=564,314&br_px=2858,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=737,334)

### 8. 若该选项为必选，请将「必须选择」切换为 ON。

亦可按需开启「可多选」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/b5bd6923-9363-4667-b70e-e6f6a3be6295/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,261&br_px=2293,1542&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=261,277)

### 9. 配置选项项。

・点击图片区域可上传图片；填写选项项名称与价格。<br />
※ 点击「＋」可新增选项项。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/1a796f4e-8573-4a01-8a37-a62e1a440a6d/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,634&br_px=1719,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=372,365)

### 10. 对新增项按同样方式配置。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/112d2a57-0ace-4485-81d9-67e7a37490f7/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=115,634&br_px=1835,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,393)

### 11. 选项示例：

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/b32c8870-0f1f-4926-a1ac-d65dca2d67d4/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2858,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 12. 配置完成后点击「保存」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/b94a4ccc-0c19-450a-b0d2-6510d77c486d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,634&br_px=1719,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=402,477)

## 选项与商品关联

### 13. 点击【商品列表】。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/25323980-26f5-44de-aca3-69d53c032d1f/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,5&br_px=1719,966&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=138,277)

### 14. 点击目标商品的「详情」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/00411038-601c-4281-b61a-0278742a58c1/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2840,1096&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 15. ① 点击「选项」；② 选择刚配置的选项组（如「米饭份量」）；③ 点击「添加」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/c2419abb-8979-4f74-b96c-b62e94686711/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2278,764&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 16. 确认无误后点击「保存」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-15/7ffbd700-8a21-4ce6-9109-fbb0fdbd63b1/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1686,1048&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)
