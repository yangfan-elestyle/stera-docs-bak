---
title: Marketplace-销售时间管理
---

用于设置商品/服务销售时间的功能。<br />
可依据营业时段灵活管理可售时段，便捷配置特定商品/服务的提供时间，提升运营效率。

## 安装方法

### 1. 在 Marketplace 输入「销售时间管理」进行搜索。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/9cb9d529-c72d-4a21-9809-9564e7debb3d/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=3082,1868&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=311,120)

### 2. 点击「安装」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/cf6c274f-1108-4be0-abde-a0437a993d8f/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=788,0&br_px=3082,1281&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=643,100)

## 设置「停止接单」

### 3. 在管理后台进入 门店 ＞ 门店列表。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/cfd42358-4b2f-476a-90fc-5b8c7a404e53/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=79,224)

### 4. 点击目标门店右侧的省略号，打开「停止接单」菜单。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/00e96458-2d38-4f18-9905-eebdf837c586/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1362,437&br_px=3082,1398&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=966,277)

### 5. 点击「停止接单」以停止受理。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/714c4e14-83b8-47ec-97a1-c9d7d17a6eab/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1362,573&br_px=3082,1534&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=910,277)

### 6. 查看弹窗提示，无误后点击「OK」完成停止接单。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/0322aef4-3865-41dd-b6db-fc61830c768d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=788,457&br_px=3082,1738&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=542,277)

### 7. 设置完成后，OneQR Web 端会显示如下提示。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/53daecda-04c1-486b-87c3-55219d57cf5a/screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=770,666&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

## 设置「恢复接单」

### 8. 在 门店 ＞ 门店列表，点击当前处于停止状态门店的省略号。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/a0b3c5aa-e696-4169-9809-85d05e3279b1/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=3082,1868&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1020,294)

### 9. 点击「恢复接单」完成恢复。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-09/3e012db9-8804-4a88-af37-9785c24f95aa/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1362,575&br_px=3082,1536&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=901,276)

## 配置销售时间管理

### 10. 点击目标门店的「详情」。
