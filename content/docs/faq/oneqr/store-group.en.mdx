---
title: Marketplace - Store Group
---

This feature lets you manage multiple stores as one group.<br />
It is recommended for merchants who operate multiple stores and want to manage them together.<br />
For cases like food courts where you want unified management, customers can scan a single QR code and then select the desired store.

### 1. Search for “Store Group” in Marketplace and install it.

![](/docs/737bc3a3-screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. In the admin console, click 【Store Group】 under 【Stores】 to open the store group list.

![](/docs/bb4a5d58-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click “New” to start creating a new group.

![](/docs/fb429332-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Enter “Store Group No.”, “Group Name”, and “Remarks” as needed, then click “Save”.

![](/docs/2c653d4b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click “＋Manage Stores” to select the stores to include in the group and finish the setup.

![](/docs/16ccb7e4-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
