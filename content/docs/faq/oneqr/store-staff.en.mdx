---
title: Marketplace - Store Staff
---

The Store Staff feature separates head-office and individual-store administration to enable efficient, role-based operations.<br />
Head office continues to centrally manage products, stores, orders, and payments, while store staff can manage only the stores they are assigned.<br />
You can also assign roles such as “Manager”, “Clerk”, or “View only” per staff member to control access to information and functions for smooth store operations.

### 1. In the admin console, select 【Marketplace】, enter “Store Staff” in the keyword field, search, then click the “Store Staff” plugin.

![](/docs/7b1c9cd3-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click “Install” when the button appears to install it.

![](/docs/5e1f6819-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. The 【Store Staff】 menu appears in the admin console; click it.

![](/docs/4ac73060-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click “＋New” to create a new entry.

![](/docs/5e36e392-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Enter the email address and staff name, then choose a role.

![](/docs/edeb7130-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Roles include Manager, Clerk, and View-only; select as needed.

![](/docs/02793255-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Tip: See below for the Store Staff permission list.

![](/docs/73c321e1-screenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Select the stores the assignee can manage via “Edit Stores”.

![](/docs/21e23e9e-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 8. You can select multiple stores. After selecting, click “OK” to save.

![](/docs/568927bf-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. To grant access to all stores, turn on “All Stores”.

![](/docs/5b7525ef-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. After configuration, click “Save”.

![](/docs/3d9f892f-ascreenshot.jpeg)

Tip: After creating a staff member, account details and password will be sent to the registered email address.

![](/docs/5978291a-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Log in using the account information in the email (App ID, email address, OTP).

    ※ Note: Please use the “**Store Management**” entry point.
    ※ On first login, you will be required to change the password.

![](/docs/9d28854d-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 12. After logging in, you can check your role at the top right.

![](/docs/8457f3a1-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 13. Users with the \[Manager\] role have permission for \[販売商品管理\], so the corresponding button is displayed.

    Users with the \[Clerk\] role do not have this permission, so the button is not shown.
    ※ This manual mainly introduces manager-level functions.

![](/docs/9c5f4636-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 14. Select the products to sell and click “OK” to save.

![](/docs/4e0c263c-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 15. Inventory can be managed under 【Products】; click “Inventory Management”.

![](/docs/552318ea-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 16. Click “Inbound”.

![](/docs/34fceb09-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 17. Enter the inbound quantity. You can also manage the expiration date.

![](/docs/b20b5049-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 18. After configuration, click “Save”.

![](/docs/27b0efdf-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 19. Click “Outbound” to manage outbound operations.

![](/docs/dd7605dd-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 20. Enter the outbound quantity; you can use “Remarks” as a memo.

![](/docs/8f382333-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 21. Click “Save”.

![](/docs/91941296-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 22. The “Inventory Count” feature currently provides the following three methods.

1）**Manual adjustment**: Manually adjust stock quantity and product expiration date for products within the specified store.<br />
2）**Inventory count with an external scanner**: Use a barcode scanner to confirm and adjust inventory.<br />
3）**CSV file import**: Upload a CSV file to perform bulk inventory adjustments.

For details, see the “Inventory Count” manual below.<br />
