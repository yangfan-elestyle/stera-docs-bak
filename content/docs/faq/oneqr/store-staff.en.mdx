---
title: Marketplace - Store Staff
---

The Store Staff feature separates HQ and individual store operations, enabling efficient, role-based management.<br />
HQ continues to manage products, stores, orders, and payments, while store staff manage only the stores they are assigned to.<br />
You can assign roles such as “Manager”, “Staff”, and “View Only” to control access appropriately and streamline store operations.

### 1. In the admin console, select 【Marketplace】 and search for “Store Staff”. Click the “Store Staff” plugin when it appears.
