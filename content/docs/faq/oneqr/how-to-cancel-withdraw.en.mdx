---
title: How to Cancel or Close Your Store
---

This guide outlines the simple process to cancel the service or close an account. It also explains how to reopen a store after it has been closed.

## Close (Cancel) a Store

### 1. Select Store

In the admin console, click “Stores”.

![](/docs/95a4ecc6-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click “Store List”.

![](/docs/ce39989f-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Select the store you wish to close and click “Details”.

![](/docs/8c2cf9e9-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Scroll to the bottom and change the status.

Click “Unpublish”.

![](/docs/7e58d5c7-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Confirm that the status has changed to “Unpublished”.

![](/docs/3001dea6-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Click “Store List” again and verify the publication status.

![](/docs/e35fb488-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Once the store status is confirmed as “Unpublished”, the cancellation is complete.

![](/docs/5fefc63c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Alert: Note:

Even after setting the store to “Unpublished”, the service fee for the current month will still be charged; starting the following month, no fees will be incurred.

Example: If you set the store to “Unpublished” on November 20, fees for November will be charged, but no fees will be charged for December.<br />
Note that the November fees will be invoiced in December.

## Reopen a Store

### 8. Click “Details” for the store you want to publish again.

![](/docs/12befee7-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Scroll to the bottom and click “Publish”.

![](/docs/bfc4847f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. After confirming the status is “Published”, reopening is complete.

    ※ Note: Once set to “Published”, fees accrue starting in the current month.

![](/docs/7d4cf07f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
