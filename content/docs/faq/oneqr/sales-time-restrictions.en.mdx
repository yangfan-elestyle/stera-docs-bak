---
title: Category-Based Sales Time Settings
---

This guide explains how to configure sales time windows per category in OneQR.

### 1. In the admin console, click “Marketplace”.

![](/docs/05ba6152-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Search for “Category” and click “Install”.

![](/docs/b9b93717-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click “Install”.

![](/docs/a889c04c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. From the Products menu, click 【Category】.

![](/docs/ee56888c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click “＋New”.

![](/docs/a967f062-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Enter the category name as needed.

※ Category code is optional.

![](/docs/b617f2a0-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. To restrict sales time, toggle “Sales Time Settings” to ON.

![](/docs/22c270d2-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Click “Edit” to configure sales time per day of week.

![](/docs/71499411-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 9. For each day, choose “Allowed”, “Not Allowed”, or set time ranges as needed, then click “OK”.

![](/docs/aa0df582-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 10. Selecting “Specify Sales Time” lets you configure time slots.

![](/docs/8ee2c340-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Select the sales time ranges.

![](/docs/e996ebe3-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 12. Click “OK” to save the settings.

![](/docs/39764967-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 13. Review the configured days/time and click “Save”.

![](/docs/a37c1e32-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 14. From Products ＞ 【Category】 again,

    You can confirm the newly configured category name.
    The current status is “Draft”, so it is not yet published.

![](/docs/86be54a9-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 15. To publish, click “Details” for the target category.

![](/docs/b740d7d7-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 16. Click “Publish” to complete the configuration.

![](/docs/652fa246-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 17. When the category status shows “Published”, it is active.

![](/docs/a677e802-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 18. You can confirm categories in the app.

    The configured sales availability applies to the specified time ranges.

![](/docs/ddf4984f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
