---
title: Marketplace - Customer Reception Number
---

This feature issues a reception number for each order and shows it to the customer, helping you manage queues smoothly.

### 1. In the admin console, click 【Marketplace】.

![](/docs/ff2c394e-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Enter “Customer Reception Number” in the keyword field and search.

![](/docs/d6ac8dc7-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Usually an “Install” button is shown. Click it to install.

※ If the plugin does not appear in search results, individual configuration is required. Please contact us below.<br />
[Support Center](https://support.elepay.io/kb-tickets/new)

![](/docs/c069d51f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. After installation, click the 【Stores】 menu in the admin console.

![](/docs/bfa6e5db-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click “Details” for the store you want to configure.

![](/docs/bfec66fd-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. If the top menu bar is partially hidden, click the right arrow to scroll.

![](/docs/34684283-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Click ［Feature Settings］.

![](/docs/b65a25f5-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 8. The “Customer Reception Number” setting appears and you can configure an order number prefix.

※ The default is ［No Prefix］.

![](/docs/99fe66da-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. To set a prefix, click ［Add Prefix］ and enter the prefix characters.

When a prefix is set, it appears at the beginning of the store’s order numbers.<br />
※ The maximum length is 2 characters.

![](/docs/b99002e4-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. After entering the prefix, click “Save” to complete the setup.

![](/docs/43b19a37-ascreenshot.jpeg)
