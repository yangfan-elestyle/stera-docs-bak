---
title: Deposit List and Deposit Settings
---

This guide explains how to manage deposit settings and check transaction data in the Elepay dashboard. You can quickly search and review deposit history for efficient operations.

## How to Use the Deposit List

### 1. In the elepay admin console, click “Deposit Management” and select “Deposit List”.

![](/docs/181595da-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Select the target period of deposits you want to review.

![](/docs/f2b88a08-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. You can choose quick filters such as “Last 7 days”, “Last 30 days”, or “This Week”, or specify a custom range. Click “Apply” after selection.

![](/docs/248f6347-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click “Search” at the top right.

![](/docs/661ebeaa-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. The deposit list for the specified period is displayed.

Click “Details” to view a breakdown of each deposit.

![](/docs/9590f944-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Under “Transaction Data”, you can view detailed transaction information.

![](/docs/9cef6b5b-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Use “CSV Export” to download CSV data.

![](/docs/3972b520-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## How to Use Deposit Settings

### 8. In the elepay admin console, select “Deposit Management”, then click “Deposit Settings” to view your registered payout bank account information.

![](/docs/6bc7b1d5-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 9. If you wish to change the bank account, please submit your request via the merchant-only inquiry form below and include the change details.

[Merchant Inquiry Form](https://support.elepay.io/kb-tickets/new)

![](/docs/9bf42c3b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
