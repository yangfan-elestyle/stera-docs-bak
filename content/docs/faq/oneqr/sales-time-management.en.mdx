---
title: Marketplace - Sales Time Management
---

Feature to configure sales time for products and services.<br />
Based on business hours, you can flexibly manage sellable time windows. Easily set availability for specific products or services to streamline operations.

## How to Install

### 1. In Marketplace, enter “Sales Time Management” and search.

![](/docs/5f357da3-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click “Install”.

![](/docs/74580a9f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## How to Configure “Stop Accepting Orders”

### 3. In the admin console, go to Stores ＞ Store List.

![](/docs/c640dcbd-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click the three-dot icon on the right of the target store to open the “Stop Accepting Orders” menu.

![](/docs/84e2eba0-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Click “Stop Accepting Orders” to apply the stop.

![](/docs/1fd42ba8-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Review the popup notice and, if acceptable, click “OK” to stop accepting orders.

![](/docs/b69ec7fe-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. After configuration, OneQR Web shows the following indication.

![](/docs/29b170a8-screenshot.jpeg)

## How to Resume Accepting Orders

### 8. In the admin console, go to Stores ＞ Store List and click the three dots to the right of a store that is currently stopped.

![](/docs/032248f5-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Click “Resume Accepting Orders” to complete resuming.

![](/docs/97863972-ascreenshot.jpeg)

## How to Configure Sales Time Management

### 10. Click “Details” for the target store.

![](/docs/982dc0fd-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Click ［Feature Settings］.

![](/docs/b51827b6-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 12. Turning ON “Stop Accepting Orders” here has the same effect as Steps 3–7 in this manual.

![](/docs/61a2ef90-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 13. Enabling ［Operating Schedule］ lets you set closed days and business hours per day of week.

![](/docs/e9ca99f6-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 14. You can configure sales time for each day. Click “Edit” to set it up.

![](/docs/6a3636ea-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 15. For sales time, choose “24-hour Sales”, “Closed”, or “Specify Sales Time”.

![](/docs/c3e614ee-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 16. After selecting as needed, click “OK”.

![](/docs/54ffd34e-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 17. Selecting “Specify Sales Time” and clicking “OK” lets you configure time slots.

![](/docs/068f68fe-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 18. You can select the sales start and end times.

![](/docs/422e1af0-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 19. Click “➕” to add multiple time slots.

![](/docs/519b8ed4-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 20. After setting multiple time slots, click “OK” to save.

    1) You can add up to 5 time slots.
    2) Cross-day ranges are supported. Example: For 14:00–02:31, business hours run from 14:00 of the same day to 02:31 of the next day.
    3) Outside business hours, OneQR Web shows a “Closed” state (see Step 7).

![](/docs/04d435a6-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 21. You can set the Last Order time to ⚪︎ minutes before closing.

    ※ Last Order time = Closing time − Last Order lead time.
    Example: If business hours are 10:00–18:00 and the lead time is set to 60 minutes before closing, the Last Order time is 17:00.

![](/docs/14d2e584-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 22. The closing-time notice appears as shown below.

![](/docs/6a810b09-screenshot.jpeg)

### 23. Sales blackout period: You can specify a particular day or a date range.

    ※ On the specified dates, the store is set to Closed.
    Examples: year-end and New Year holidays, Golden Week, etc.

![](/docs/d09af5c6-ascreenshot.jpeg)
