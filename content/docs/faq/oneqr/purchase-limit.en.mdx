---
title: Marketplace - Purchase Limits
---

This guide introduces the feature to apply purchase limits to specific products. It is useful for age-restricted items such as alcoholic beverages.

Age restriction:<br />
Verify the customer’s age at purchase and restrict as necessary to prevent underage purchases of alcohol.<br />
Easy override:<br />
Restrictions can be easily lifted after age verification or when specific conditions are met.

## Configuration

### 1. In the admin console, click 【Products】.

### 2. Click ［Purchase Limit Check］.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-08-05/43fef8be-48c4-46da-ad80-e1ad3491827d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,443&br_px=1719,1404&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=74,276)

### 3. Upload the restriction image and select a verification/override method.

There are three override methods; choose one.

**Method 1: Staff QR Code**<br />
Issue a specific QR code to staff; scan it to override at checkout.

To issue a staff QR code, click the QR code option under item 2.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-08-05/ad361f3d-3947-4612-b219-a9ac6662d4aa/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,61&br_px=1214,830&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

## OneQR Shop Examples

### 11. Staff QR override:

    ① A cart item flagged with 【Purchase Limit】 is added.

② Clicking 【Pay】 shows a confirmation popup.

③ Click “Yes” to open the scanner and scan the staff QR code.

④ Proceed to the payment method selection screen.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-29/78da437e-52cd-4d07-a1b4-b9631da413f2/screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1174,486&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 12. With override password:

    ① A cart item flagged with 【Purchase Limit】 is added.

② Clicking 【Pay】 shows a confirmation popup.

③ Since a verification password is configured, click 【Confirm】 to open the password entry screen.

④ If the password is correct, proceed to the payment method selection screen.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-01-29/51045262-ded8-4f44-969c-dbab4cdeadc6/screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=4096,1214&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 13. Without override password:

    ① A cart item flagged with 【Purchase Limit】 is added.

② Clicking 【Pay】 shows a confirmation popup.

③ If no verification password is configured, clicking 【Confirm】 proceeds directly to payment method selection.
