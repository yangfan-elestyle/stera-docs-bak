---
title: Marketplace-门店员工
---

门店员工功能用于将总部与各门店的管理职责分离，实现高效、基于角色的运营。<br />
总部继续负责商品、门店、订单、支付等整体管理；门店员工仅管理其被分配的门店。<br />
可为员工设置「店长」「店员」「仅查看」等权限，以适当控制访问范围并优化门店运营。

### 1. 在管理后台选择【Marketplace】，搜索「门店员工」，出现插件后点击进入。

### 2. 点击「安装」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-06/b86d5460-a104-40c6-9d7a-fc810b97a6b9/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=115,0&br_px=2868,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=755,77)

### 3. 管理后台将显示【门店员工】菜单，点击进入。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-06/3dadc6ff-93dc-4650-b6c0-867571fccdc6/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2752,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=34,66)

### 4. 点击「＋新建」创建员工。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-06/c25ccb85-9bf0-4eba-b97f-c30714a4db95/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=115,0&br_px=2868,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=958,106)

### 5. 输入邮箱与员工姓名，并选择角色。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-06/63c3f3c8-8ca0-406c-bd4a-a5df462e7d5e/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=70,0&br_px=2822,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,217)

### 6. 角色包括店长、店员与仅查看，可按需选择。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-06/b32c3fe2-9275-4f5c-a955-26b71a404f8d/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=57,28&br_px=2810,1567&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-06/f8fe411e-c6d0-4025-9ec5-e2e6f1dad0e1/screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1082,828&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 7. 点击［编辑门店］选择该员工可管理的门店。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-06/bc0d9081-f39a-479f-ac0c-5b43a5409552/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,57&br_px=2752,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=379,302)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-06/0878ada3-ed6d-4a43-90f2-0bf6e3f7f4e8/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=115,57&br_px=2868,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=872,523)
