---
title: How to Use the Easy Payment Invoice Feature
---

This page explains how merchants can bill their customers using the Easy Payment invoice feature.

Tip: This section introduces how merchants can bill their own customers using the Easy Payment feature.

### 1. In the elepay admin console, click “Multi-Payment”.

![](/docs/bd194b41-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Under “Easy Payment”, click “Invoice Payment”.

![](/docs/93018746-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click “＋New”.

![](/docs/a6d60ad4-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Enter subject, customer, due date, etc., then click “Save”.

※ “＋New Customer” lets you register customer information.<br />
※ Click “＋” to add line items.<br />
※ The memo field is for internal notes and is not shown to customers.

![](/docs/afeaf91e-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. After entering the required information, click “Send”.

![](/docs/4caed2cf-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Review the invoice, and if all is correct, click “OK”.

![](/docs/3b1b9d56-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 7. After the invoice is finalized, clicking “Send” lets you deliver it to the customer via three methods. Select the appropriate method and click “OK”.

1. “Copy URL”: copy the invoice URL and send it via SMS, etc.
2. “Quick Email”: send the invoice to the customer’s email address.
3. “QR Code”: send the QR image or print and mail it.

![](/docs/7e05eaab-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Tip: After sending, click “OK” to update the invoice status to “Sent”.

## Check Payment Status

### 8. When an invoice status shows “Paid”, click “Details” to see the invoice details.

![](/docs/d3026b39-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. For paid invoices, a “Payment” section is displayed, where you can view payment method and refund information.

![](/docs/6c74424f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Alert: ⚠️Note⚠️

Even if a payment is refunded, the invoice status remains “Paid” and does not change to “Refunded”.

Alert: Alert!

About expired invoices

To reduce merchant workload, the elepay・OneQR dashboard allows payment of expired invoices as-is.

For an expired invoice, you have two options:

### 1. Create a new invoice using the “Copy” function

- Quickly create a new invoice based on the expired one.

### 2. Pay the expired invoice as-is

- Payment is still possible even if the invoice has expired.

Choose the method that best fits your operations.

The elepay・OneQR dashboard offers flexible options to support smooth operations. Please make full use of them.

## Edit Customer Information

### 10. To edit registered customer information, select “Customers” in the admin console and click “Details”.

![](/docs/96628a0b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Click “Edit” at the top right to view and modify the customer record.

![](/docs/ad6f7ed8-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

Tip: 【Important】<br />
The customer name is automatically used as the invoice recipient when generating an invoice. Please pay attention when entering names.<br />
If you include honorifics such as “様” or “御中” in the customer name, they will be automatically reflected on the invoice as well.
