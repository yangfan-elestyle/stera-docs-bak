---
title: Marketplace-顾客取号
---

该功能会为每笔订单生成一个取号并展示给顾客，便于顺序管理。

### 1. 在管理后台点击【Marketplace】。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/2b263801-1468-4028-8339-c11e97da9999/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,57&br_px=2752,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=21,356)

### 2. 在关键词栏输入「顾客取号」并搜索。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/7d04ab27-30b9-40b3-98d5-14d853f818eb/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2752,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=314,128)

### 3. 通常会显示「安装」按钮，点击安装。

※ 如搜索未出现该插件，说明需要单独开通，请通过下方链接联系我们。<br />
[支持中心](https://support.elepay.io/kb-tickets/new)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/03ade163-d2cc-4bda-b7c0-ec74d0bc5380/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=93,0&br_px=2846,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=802,69)

### 4. 安装完成后，在管理后台点击【门店】菜单。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/0af17128-bd45-4561-b40e-bb24d9c4a35b/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2752,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=46,82)

### 5. 点击需要设置的门店的「详情」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/e729841d-39c3-4776-acc5-d7ce52da3eea/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=93,57&br_px=2846,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=994,314)

### 6. 如顶部菜单未完全显示，点击右侧箭头滚动。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/fb1a12f5-c863-42b5-be2e-a89a6dd61d8c/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=93,0&br_px=2846,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1044,52)

### 7. 点击［功能设置］。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/352c95fa-7882-4180-a201-ce96dc32413d/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=93,0&br_px=2846,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1009,52)

### 8. 将显示顾客取号设置，并可设置订单号前缀。

※ 默认状态为［无前缀］。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/fb0572b3-73ef-4c1e-a27c-af0963d51afa/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=46,28&br_px=2799,1567&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 9. 需要设置前缀时，点击［添加前缀］并输入前缀字符。

设置后，前缀将显示在门店订单号的开头。<br />
※ 最多允许 2 个字符。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/70b571de-ddd3-4778-8bd4-85c0d054641d/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=46,28&br_px=2799,1567&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 10. 输入前缀后点击「保存」，完成设置。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-20/9e8bbb0a-02c9-4240-b07e-524398ab9069/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,50&br_px=2752,1589&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=383,277)
