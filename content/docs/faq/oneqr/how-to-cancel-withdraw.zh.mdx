---
title: 解约与退会操作指南
---

本指南介绍服务解约/退会的简要流程，并说明关闭门店后再次开店时的设置方法。

## 关闭（解约）门店

### 1. 选择门店

在管理后台点击「门店」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/a1dcd0b2-aaa4-42e8-adf7-fdaafa5c6cb3/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=83,154)

### 2. 点击「门店列表」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/ab1ea4d6-e715-4d1a-826a-ee1c7f508edf/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=89,219)

### 3. 选择要关闭的门店，点击「详情」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/df1dc423-8245-48a3-822f-64497564d332/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=3082,1868&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=985,298)

### 4. 滚动至页面底部并更改状态。

点击「设为未公开」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/ebca8213-19dd-4bc5-9e76-22faee5d8742/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=3082,1868&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=418,581)

### 5. 确认状态已切换为「未公开」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/42b47840-8830-48e4-a36f-85c7c37ab43d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=212,906&br_px=1932,1868&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,397)

### 6. 返回「门店列表」再次确认门店的公开状态。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/38943998-fe41-484f-88f0-e2c7504e29fd/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=185,214)

### 7. 确认目标门店状态已变更为「未公开」后，解约设置完成。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/65e51e43-3946-40d4-90ec-a2b57d3e7963/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=3082,1868&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=752,299)

Alert: 注意：

将门店设为「未公开」后，当月使用费仍会计费；自次月起不再计费。

例：若在 11 月 20 日设为「未公开」，则 11 月的使用费仍会计费，但 12 月将不再计费。<br />
另外，11 月使用费的账单会在 12 月开具，请留意。

## 重新公开门店

### 8. 点击需要重新公开的门店的「详情」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/68590f8e-595a-44ba-a5be-0e2d906d17cc/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=3768,1830&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 9. 滚动到页面底部并点击「公开」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/106349cc-19a2-4ae5-a705-5bad26273f99/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=3082,1868&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 10. 确认状态变更为「公开中」后，门店重新开业设置完成。

    **※ 注意：设置为「公开中」后，当月起开始计费。**

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/f1b7fde6-011f-4728-8e73-a94597534f31/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2848,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)
