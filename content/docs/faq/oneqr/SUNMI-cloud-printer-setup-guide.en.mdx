---
title: OneQR and SUNMI Cloud Printer Setup Guide
---

This guide explains how to configure OneQR with the SUNMI Cloud Printer.<br />
Once configured, order slips and receipts can be printed automatically.

## Initial Printer Registration

### 1. Download the SmartBoss app to centrally manage device settings and payment data.

![](/docs/c95f3f6b-screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Printer connection: connect the printer in SmartBoss.

1. Click ［＋］ on the printer screen.<br />
2. Follow the on-screen printer connection guide and click ［Start］.<br />
3. The connection begins.

![](/docs/4ae39c14-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Wi-Fi settings: configure the printer’s Wi-Fi.

1. Select the desired Wi‑Fi from the list.<br />
2. Enter the Wi‑Fi password and click “OK”.<br />
3. When “Printer placement completed” appears, click “OK”.<br />
4. Confirm the prompts “Printer enabled / Wi‑Fi connected successfully” to finish.

![](/docs/0d433912-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Printer setup: configure and test the printer in SmartBoss.

1. In printer setup, click “Next” and confirm that “Initial printer setup completed” is shown.<br />
2. Click “Test Print” and confirm that “Test print completed” appears.

![](/docs/08bff966-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. Make the printer public in SmartBoss.

1. Select the printer to publish.<br />
2. Click ［Change Usage Status］.<br />
3. Select ［Public］ to complete.

![](/docs/6c6e66c6-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## Register the Printer in the Admin Console

### 6. 1. In the admin console, click 【Store List】 under the Stores menu.

![](/docs/dcb91912-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 7. Click “Details” for the store you want to configure.

![](/docs/b0c196ab-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 8. Click ［Printer Management］.

![](/docs/8e66e904-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 9. Click “＋New” to configure a new printer.

![](/docs/53813010-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 10. Enter the required information.

Printer Name: set freely.<br />
Printer Number: typically the “N” number printed on the bottom (or side) of the device.

![](/docs/a786dd30-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 11. Select what to print.

- Receipt: a standard receipt.
- Receipt + Order Slip: when an order is placed on the Shop terminal, two slips print in order — a receipt and an order slip.
- Order Slip: a ticket-like order slip. Example: after ordering on the Shop terminal, the slip prints automatically, and customers present it at the counter to receive their meal.
- Kitchen Slip: a slip used by chefs. After an order is placed, it prints from the kitchen printer and is used to prepare dishes.

![](/docs/e2e9bc5b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 12. After configuring, click “OK” at the top right.

![](/docs/3eebcf74-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## Link the Printer in the Admin Console

### 13. After configuration, the status initially shows ［Not Connected］.

Click here if you want to turn the configured printer “ON” (activate) and change the status.

![](/docs/1316b976-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 14. The status is now set to “Linked”.

※ To stop linking with the printer, change the status to “Not Connected” (OFF).

![](/docs/3887eb33-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 15. Use “Test Print” to verify the actual print status and finish the setup.

![](/docs/7e301f6c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
