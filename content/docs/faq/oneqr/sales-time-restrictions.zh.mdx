---
title: 分类与销售时间限制设置
---

本文介绍如何在 OneQR 中按分类配置销售时间。

### 1. 在管理后台点击「Marketplace」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/4e16e7cf-84c1-4a91-8e1b-62339c86ac28/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,634&br_px=1719,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=118,280)

### 2. 搜索「分类」，点击「安装」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/63cd245c-5b40-4c6d-b54b-6405afd8a679/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=246,0&br_px=1965,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,246)

### 3. 点击「安装」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-11-26/184c0570-3c19-4ce4-a7fa-1fbaa8f86efe/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=556,0&br_px=2850,1281&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=766,99)

### 4. 在【商品】菜单点击【分类】。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-03-06/5633ba90-df30-4b66-82ba-dd89477dd956/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2850,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=37,232)

### 5. 点击「＋新建」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-11-26/3ca82404-5003-4688-b70f-1f58bfe097b5/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1130,0&br_px=2850,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=850,142)

### 6. 按需填写分类名称。

※ 分类编码为可选项。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/8059446f-1ede-4127-924a-d9a27c31ab93/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=370,0&br_px=2090,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,208)

### 7. 如需限制销售时间，将「销售时间设置」切换为 ON。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/7fb45e2c-c4c8-4cff-97d1-894ee2bd3a5c/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=134,145&br_px=1853,1106&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,277)

### 8. 点击「编辑」可按星期配置销售时间。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/da5e6bbd-0c12-40fc-8038-076d04b5af02/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=556,67&br_px=2850,1348&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=655,277)

### 9. 逐日选择「允许」「不允许」或设置时间段，完成后点击「OK」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/f4f6a30c-101d-42f7-9ef9-17166f62dc10/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=556,314&br_px=2850,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=619,277)

### 10. 选择「指定销售时间」可配置时间段。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/8ac7448c-333d-4a8a-bbf9-507b61d816ae/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=360,291&br_px=2080,1252&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,277)

### 11. 选择需要的销售时间段。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/0ffba71f-6f51-44aa-a3ca-1bcdf44b8d24/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=256,525&br_px=1976,1486&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,276)

### 12. 点击「OK」保存设置。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/0d1022b3-af7d-4061-9d9c-585ede18c3f1/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=556,314&br_px=2850,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=621,366)

### 13. 核对已配置的星期与时间段，点击「保存」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/b67ad438-376d-4777-a16d-18be7ab43b62/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,314&br_px=2293,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=449,447)

### 14. 返回【商品】＞【分类】，

    可看到刚配置的分类名称。
    当前状态为「草稿」，尚未公开。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-03-06/779496ce-b6b8-46df-b5ea-f1152a051423/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2850,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=312,294)

### 15. 如需公开，点击目标分类的「详情」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-12-19/07357ade-5214-404e-8823-af2043b80336/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2850,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=981,290)

### 16. 点击「公开」完成设置。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/0fb955f1-bb16-4133-9f45-604818b19b72/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=352,634&br_px=2072,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,489)

### 17. 当分类状态显示为「公开中」即表示已上线。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-16/52a62bf6-7f88-4059-8902-e72084e83548/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2774,612&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 18. 可在 App 端验证分类展示，

    已配置的销售可售/不可售时间将按设定生效。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-03-06/8a0ea198-6216-4b22-b42d-9abe76721077/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1082,1594&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=760&wat_scale=67&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1298,27)
