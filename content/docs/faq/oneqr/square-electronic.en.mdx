---
title: Apply for Square Electronic Money
---

Currently, it takes about 4–9 days from application to activation for all e-money brands. Once activation is completed, a notification email is sent for each brand. You can check the latest status in Square Data under your store information.

### 1. Enable electronic money

To accept electronic money payments, after creating a Square account you must complete activation for each store.

### 2. In Square Data, go to \[Store Information\], then under “Payment Brands” click \[Enable\].

Note: The items shown may vary depending on your account usage.<br />
Under “Activation Status by Brand”, you can check each brand’s status.

### 3. Select \[Enable all electronic money\] to enable all brands at once.

Some merchants may be asked to submit additional information.

### 4. Each brand’s status changes to “Pending (X–X business days)”.

### 5. When the activation status is updated, you will receive an email notification. You can also check the latest status under Store Information ＞ \[Payment Brands\].

Tip: ### Important Notes

- Before using electronic money payments, activation is required per store in your Square account.

- Activating electronic money requires applying for JCB usage.

- Charities and non-profit organizations cannot accept electronic money payments.

- To accept electronic money payments, [**compatible hardware and software**](https://squareup.com/help/jp/ja/article/5584-devices-compatible-with-square-contactless-and-chip-card-reader) are required.
