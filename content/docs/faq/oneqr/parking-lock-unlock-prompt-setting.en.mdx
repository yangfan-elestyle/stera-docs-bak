---
title: Parking Lock Unlock Prompt Settings
---

This guide explains how to configure the prompt shown in parking lots: “After payment is completed, please confirm that the lock has been unlocked before exiting.”

### 1. Select the target parking lot and click “OneQR”.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-06-13/a1d6a010-f883-4d28-b109-fa0ce6e94528/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1120,626&force_format=jpeg&q=100)

### 2. Click “OneQR Store” from the left menu.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-06-12/92e1d8e0-0676-485a-9154-1e4ad7df8fc5/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,534&br_px=1719,1496&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=91,388)

### 3. Click “Parking-related Settings”.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-06-12/4659ba58-b222-4bf9-acda-a6de88eb0305/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=505,220)

### 4. Turn on the “Unlock Confirmation Message” toggle and click “Save” to complete the setup.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-06-12/d6b447eb-613d-44b6-ae0e-a7f1b097e5f6/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,214&br_px=2150,1496&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=559,543)
