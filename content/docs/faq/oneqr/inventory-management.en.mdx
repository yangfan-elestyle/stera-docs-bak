title: Marketplace - Inventory Management
---

Using Inventory Management, you can instantly check product stock status. When stock falls below the safety stock, you receive automatic notifications. Based on theoretical stock, you can view the most accurate inventory status. This reduces the risk of stockouts and enables smooth sales operations.

## Install and Configure Inventory Management

### 1. In Marketplace, search the keyword “Inventory Management”, then click 【Inventory Management】 when it appears.

![](/docs/274fbace-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click “Install”.

![](/docs/f039a9b0-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Configure according to your business needs.

- Sold-out display: When enabled, items with no stock show “Sold Out” and cannot be purchased.<br />
- Set standard stock: Turning the switch ON lets you set the theoretical stock as the standard stock in one click.

![](/docs/22a5e116-screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. After configuration, click “Confirm”.

![](/docs/ac031fa1-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. When the setup completes, click “Start Using”.

![](/docs/9d297e6f-ascreenshot.jpeg)

## Inventory Count

Tip: Within a specified store, you can adjust inventory for products in that store.<br />
Currently, the following three methods are available.

1) **Manual adjustment**: Manually adjust inventory quantity and product expiration date for products in the specified store.<br />
2) **Inventory count with an external scanner**: Use a barcode scanner to confirm and adjust inventory.<br />
3) **CSV file import**: Upload a CSV file to adjust inventory in bulk.

### 25. 1) Manual adjustment:

    Click “Manual Input” under Inventory Count.

![](/docs/65227fab-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 26. Enter the stock quantity. You can also specify an expiration date.

![](/docs/e436820e-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 27. After configuration, click “Save”.

![](/docs/077426d1-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 28. Review the popup and click “OK”.

![](/docs/960c15f4-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 29. In “Details” for the configured product, you can view inventory history details.

![](/docs/56681019-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 30. Inventory history details:

![](/docs/9c9c9e82-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 31. 2) **Inventory count with an external scanner**:

    1) Pair the scanner with your PC.
    ※ For Bluetooth, connect to the PC and pair it.
    ※ For a USB scanner, insert the USB into the PC and pair it.

    2) In the admin screen, click 【Inventory Count】 → “Barcode Scan” to switch to scan mode.

![](/docs/3b031215-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 32. 3) Scan the product JAN code or custom product code with the scanner. The quantity displays on the PC screen (in red), and a “beep” sounds.

    Confirm the post-scan stock quantity, then click the “Save” button at the top right.
    ※ In scan mode, you cannot modify the quantity by manual input.

![](/docs/ab1509d9-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 33. 3) **CSV file import**:

    1) From 【CSV Export】, select “Store Inventory CSV”, export the CSV file, and edit it.
    ※ The settings icon on the right lets you configure which fields to include.

![](/docs/2dc3591c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 34. CSV template configuration:

    1. To include a field from the left (Non-output) list, check it.
    2. Click the center arrow “→”.
    3. Click “Save”.

    Note: To move output items to the Non-output list, check items in the right box, click “←”, then click “Save”.

![](/docs/84ff75ed-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 35. Click 【Inventory Count】 → “CSV Import & Update”.

![](/docs/0eee6aa0-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 36. A popup prompts you to upload a CSV file.

    3) Click “Choose File” and upload the CSV you edited in step 1).

![](/docs/53b60e02-screenshot.jpeg)

Alert: Alert!<br />
There are three constraints when uploading:

※ Save the edited Excel as a **CSV file (encoding: UTF-8 with BOM)**.<br />
※ The CSV file can contain up to **1000 rows**.<br />
※ The CSV must include the 【Product ID】 field; otherwise parsing will fail.<br />
You can also adjust values for these three fields: **【Theoretical Expiration Date】, 【Standard Stock】, 【Theoretical Stock】**.

### 37. 4) Review the data, adjust as needed, and click **“Save”** to apply the updates.

### 38. If the uploaded file meets the required format, the status shows **“CSV Parse Succeeded”**.

    If requirements are not met, **“CSV Parse Failed”** is shown.

Only when **“CSV Parse Succeeded”** is shown will the **“Next”** button appear; click it to proceed to the edit confirmation page.

![](/docs/e05c879d-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 39. Example of **“CSV Parse Failed”**:

    If parsing fails, click “Delete” to remove the file, confirm the file format, and try again.

![](/docs/5ecf8d18-screenshot.jpeg)

### 40. Example of the confirmation page after importing a CSV file:

    1) On this page, you can continue adjusting these three items: **【Theoretical Expiration Date】, 【Standard Stock】, 【Theoretical Stock】**.
    2) Click “Save” at the top right to apply changes.

※ You can check history per product in 【Inventory History Details】. For details, see steps 24 and 25 in this manual.

![](/docs/13b879b8-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## Settings

### 41. From the admin screen, click 【Settings】 in the Inventory Management menu.

![](/docs/a514b1c4-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 42. In 【Alert Conditions】, set the “Safety Line” to enable alert notifications.

    ※ By default, alert notifications are OFF; “No” is selected in Alert Conditions.
    ※ These settings apply to all stores; please note this scope.

![](/docs/962a1c2b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 43. An alert is triggered when any of the following three conditions is met:

- **【Product Safety Stock】**: Sends a notification when a product’s stock falls below the specified safety stock.
- **【Store Safety Stock】**: Sends a notification when the store’s total stock falls below the specified value.
- **【Days Until Expiry】**: Sends an alert when a product’s expiration date approaches the specified number of days.

![](/docs/475e6475-screenshot.jpeg)

### 44. Notification methods support Email and Slack Webhook configuration.

![](/docs/91062928-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 45. After configuration, click “Save”.

![](/docs/d861b85f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

![](/docs/8f6c4bff-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 47. Click “Details” for the store you want to configure.

![](/docs/1937c707-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
