title: Marketplace-商品详情设置
---

用于为单个商品配置详细属性的功能。本文介绍套装销售、购买限制、公开时间等细粒度设置的方法。

## 用餐方式设置（堂食/外带）

### 1. 在【商品】菜单点击【商品列表】。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-26/a3f7de9c-5327-4936-bfc5-70a380e74659/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2752,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=77,167)

### 2. 点击目标商品的「详情」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-26/cd9fe481-5491-45ad-af63-70b7df333d00/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=93,57&br_px=2846,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1000,312)

### 3. 点击［详细设置］。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-26/536d573e-0e3d-4dd6-beee-e20047a8ac8d/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2752,1538&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=509,52)

### 4. 点击［用餐方式］，下拉选择「仅堂食/仅外带」。

※ 默认为［不限制］，即不限制堂食或外带。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-26/8551b938-a75c-430f-a637-b2aef4c647a6/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,12&br_px=2752,1551&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=301,277)

### 5. 点击 \[每单购买数量限制\] 可限制每笔订单的购买数量。

※ 提供 1～9 的 9 个选项，表示每单可购买的最大数量；最小为 1，最大为 9。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-26/23a3a6b3-055a-41d5-985a-54b379d99138/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,57&br_px=2088,1596&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=380,500)

### 6. 示例：如下设置表示：

・该商品仅支持堂食购买，不可外带。<br />
・每笔订单最多可购买 1 件。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-02-26/c5b3f0eb-4a7b-46ae-bb34-808b08ff96e1/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=83,0&br_px=1897,948&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)
