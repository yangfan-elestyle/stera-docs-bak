---
title: 入金列表与入金设置
---

本指南介绍如何在 Elepay 控制台管理入金设置并查看交易数据。你可以便捷地搜索与查看入金历史，从而高效完成日常操作。

## 入金列表的使用方法

### 1. 在 elepay 管理后台点击「入金管理」，选择「入金列表」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-09/43c94904-0c5c-431b-a2ad-1dabd75066e5/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=148,233)

### 2. 选择要查看的入金时间区间。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-09/d044e9a1-b533-4f81-b508-b35ce3149cdf/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=517,257)

### 3. 可选择「过去 7 天」「过去 30 天」「本周」等快捷选项，或自定义日期范围，选择后点击「确定」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-09/73f21617-df21-434b-aa12-fb494b97c193/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=388,569&br_px=2108,1530&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=546,276)

### 4. 点击右上角「搜索」。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-11-28/bbc1fdc4-7c47-44b1-ad58-775558bbe0cb/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=388,0&br_px=2108,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=835,210)

### 5. 将显示指定时间段内的入金列表。

点击「详情」可查看入金明细。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-11-28/5cde7115-45d8-4615-9bf2-46c5ace17a88/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2108,1281&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1004,266)

### 6. 在「交易数据」中可查看交易详细信息。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-09/254be237-d84d-4905-8215-2c576beba99d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1719,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=301,103)

### 7. 通过「CSV Export」可下载 CSV 数据。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-11-28/3d093192-ccbc-46cc-ad4e-6c22de26e237/user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2108,1281&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1004,83)

## 入金设置的使用方法

### 8. 在 elepay 管理后台选择「入金管理」后，点击「入金设置」即可查看已登记的入金银行账户信息。

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2024-10-09/496cf530-c926-423d-afae-d070cf6f177d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,21&br_px=1719,982&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=141,277)

### 9. 如需变更账户，请通过下方商户专用咨询表单提交申请并填写变更内容。

[商户专用咨询表单](https://support.elepay.io/kb-tickets/new)
