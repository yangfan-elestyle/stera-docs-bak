title: Marketplace - Product Detail Settings
---

This feature lets you configure detailed attributes per product. This guide explains how to set up options such as set/bundle sales, sales restrictions, and publication date/time.

## Dining Option Settings (Dine-in / Takeout)

### 1. In the product menu, click 【Product List】.

![](/docs/16344ef3-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 2. Click “Details” for the target product.

![](/docs/7217f882-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 3. Click [Advanced Settings].

![](/docs/cace048c-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 4. Click [Meal Selection], then choose “Dine-in only / Takeout only” from the dropdown.

Note: The default is [No restriction], so there is no limitation on dine-in or takeout.

![](/docs/0f7e1c4b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 5. \[Limit purchase quantity per order\] lets you set a per-order quantity limit for the product.

There are nine options from 1 to 9, indicating the maximum quantity allowed per order. The minimum limit is 1 and the maximum is 9.

![](/docs/9f112d88-ascreenshot.jpeg)

### 6. Example: The following settings mean:

・This product can be purchased only for dine-in and is not available for takeout.<br />
・Only one item can be purchased per order for this product.<br />

![](/docs/a8dd6ab0-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

## How to Configure Set-Sale Only

### 20. Refer to Steps 1–3 of this manual (Products > Product List > Details).

    Click [Advanced Settings] > [Set-Sale Only].

    The dropdown will appear.
    1) Default is 【No restriction】. The product can be sold as a single item and as part of a set.
    2) If set to 【Set-sale only】, this product can be sold only as part of a set and cannot be sold individually.
    3) If the product itself is a 【Product Set】, this setting does not apply and the default is 【No restriction】.

![](/docs/b3a05042-ascreenshot.jpeg)

## How to Configure Sales Restriction Control

### 22. Click [Sales Restriction Control].

![](/docs/dcd51bb9-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 23. The dropdown will appear.

    1) [Do not sell when stock is 0]: When stock is 0, the product will no longer be displayed in the product list.
    2) [Do not sell when the best-before date has expired]: When the product has passed its best-before date, it will no longer be displayed in the product list.
    3) [Do not sell when stock is 0 or the best-before date has expired]: If either applies (stock 0 or past best-before date), the product will not be displayed in the product list.

![](/docs/c872ebc3-screenshot.jpeg)

### 24. Example:

    The label appears on all devices on the Mobile/POS/Shop sides.
    If the purchase limit is exceeded, an error message needs to be displayed.

![](/docs/bd156da6-screenshot.jpeg)

## How to Configure Publication/Sales Date and Time

### 25. Configure under [Product Display Scheduling].

    Default value: No restriction. Display time is not controlled; once the product is published, it will be displayed automatically.

![](/docs/c136163b-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 26. Click [Product Display Scheduling], then select [Display at specified date/time], and click it.

![](/docs/c1e9f9f8-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 27. Click [Select date].

![](/docs/58ccbc46-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)

### 28. The calendar is displayed. Select the desired date/time and click “Confirm”.

    Finally, review the contents and click “Save” to complete the settings.

![](/docs/279a5a5f-user_cropped_screenshot.jpeg)
